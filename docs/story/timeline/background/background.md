## The Post Truth Era

Thanks to the internet and scores of TV channels catering to single points of view, it was very possible for people  
to consume the content that agreed with their beliefs and sensibilities.  Huge swaths of the public lived in vast echo  
chambers where alternative points of view were never challenged, or simply never even heard of.  Diversity came to  
mean "superficial differences, as long as you tow the party line".  

Tech giants would watch your searches and online posts, tracking users to provide similar interests and like minded
peop;e, further strengthening the echo chamber.  And to add insult to injury, companies could purchase your browsing 
data to target users for goods and services.
