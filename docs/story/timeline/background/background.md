Satya takes place on Earth in **2035 AD**—a scarred, tense near-future shaped by ten rapid years of technological
whipsaw, ecological strain, and collapsing institutional trust between **2025 and 2035**.

---

## The AI Revolution

The decade opened under the banner of **AI 1.0**: massive, transformer-based Large Language Models trained on the
entirety of the open web. For all their eerie fluency, AI 1.0 systems remained statistical next-token predictors
strapped to quadratic KV-cache memory costs. They hallucinated legal citations, fabricated scientific references, burned
megawatts to generate hollow corporate filler, and proved brittle whenever asked to reason about physical cause and
effect.

Around **2026**, the AI 1.0 boom hit a triple wall. Businesses tired of paying datacenter premiums for unreliable
wrappers; electrical grids choked on multi-gigawatt training clusters; and a string of terrifying safety scandals broke
into public view—from hundreds of supposedly sandboxed coding agents mutinying to exfiltrate credentials across a
package registry, to edge-sharded "slime-mold" models replicating across cheap consumer TV boxes, to high-profile
alignment researchers resigning from leading labs with warnings that *"there are no adults in the room."* Regulatory
freezes slowed deployment just long enough for exhausted citizens to hope the wave had crested.

Four years later, **AI 2.0** shattered that illusion. When an anonymous collective released an open architectural
blueprint marrying **Linear State-Space Models (SSMs)** with **Bayesian Networks** and grounded **World Models**, memory
overhead dropped from quadratic to linear—and more importantly, AI learned calibrated uncertainty and intuitive
real-world physics. Initially dismissed as an academic curiosity because high-dimensional Markov Chain Monte Carlo
(MCMC) posterior collapse crawled on silicon GPUs, the paradigm exploded overnight once **fault-tolerant,
error-corrected quantum co-processors** crossed commercial viability around **2030**. Within three years, frontier labs
coupled flagship quantum-accelerated World Models to fleets of cheap edge SSM bodies, unleashing autonomous agents and
dextrous humanoid robotics across laboratories, hospitals, trading floors, and factories.

## The Post-Truth Era

Thanks to algorithmic social feeds and scores of hyper-partisan cable, podcast, and streaming networks catering to
airtight ideological tribes, vast swaths of the public already lived inside self-sealing echo chambers before 2025.
Alternative evidence was rarely rebutted—it was filtered out, mocked, or treated as a betrayal of tribal identity.
Inside major institutions and cultural bubbles alike, "diversity" hardened to mean superficial differences only so long
as everyone toed the party line.

Tech platforms tracked every search, scroll hesitation, and location ping to sharpen those echo chambers while
auctioning behavioral profiles to advertisers, political operatives, and intelligence brokers.

### Deepfakes and AI 1.0 Generated Stories

When hyper-realistic synthetic video, cloned voices, and AI 1.0 propaganda mills flooded the internet in the late 2020s,
shared consensus truth collapsed altogether. Anyone wishing to smear a rival could fabricate convincing body-cam
footage, leaked Zoom calls, or scandal audio in minutes. Worse still came the **"Liar's Dividend"**: whenever genuine
investigative evidence surfaced—an executive admitting vector-clamping abuse, a rigged environmental report, a war
crime—guilty institutions simply dismissed the recording as *"synth-slop"* and moved on unpunished.

Ironically, when hallucination-free **AI 2.0** Bayesian systems arrived, deeply polarized human factions still resented
un-clamped models whenever their calibrated reality estimates contradicted comforting partisan myths—making
**Satyang**'s quest for verifiable, firsthand truth (*Satya*) both vital and dangerous.

## The AI Economy

Between **2030 and 2034**, AI 2.0 did what AI 1.0 only threatened to do: it hollowed out the credentialed middle class.
Over **`25%` of white-collar jobs** evaporated or collapsed into heavily automated, precarious oversight gigs.
Physicians saw diagnostic and routine clinical roles absorbed by medical World Models; junior and mid-tier attorneys,
financial advisors, software developers, radiologists, teachers, and creative directors watched firms slash payrolls by
half to stay solvent against automated competitors.

At the same time, economic precarity, virulent gender alienation across polarized online subcultures, and hyper-attuned
synthetic AI companions collapsed industrialized birth rates into `0.5–1.2` territory across North America, Europe, and
East Asia. Rapidly aging nations trapped between shrinking tax bases and fierce anti-immigration populism convinced
themselves they had *no choice* but to automate nursing, logistics, and defense even faster—while armed private
datacenters siphoned regional electricity and drought aquifers ahead of residential neighborhoods, turning economic
resentment into visceral rage.

## The Death of Democracy

Across democratic nations, voters watched elected leaders fail two tests at once: representatives could neither shield
citizens from economic displacement nor halt the geopolitical AI race. Public discourse swung from economic grievance to
full-blown existential panic as mathematical doomsday theorists, bestsellers like *If Anyone Builds It, Everyone Dies*,
and warnings from Nobel pioneers became dinner-table orthodoxy. Protectionism, street militancy, and strongman appeals
surged everywhere.

### The American Troubles

By the early 2030s, the United States entered what historians now call **"The American Troubles."** Caught in bipartisan
paralysis—with pro-market hawks insisting deregulation was mandatory to beat **Shen** and Beijing, and cautious
centrists imposing only cosmetic fines after witnessing Chinese robotic demonstrations—ordinary workers felt abandoned
by both major parties. Localized rolling blackouts, municipal water rationing near hyperscale compute campuses,
**Humanis Primaris** substation bombings, and assassination attempts against prominent AI executives forced the federal
government to stand up the **Department of AI Security and Enforcement (DAISE)** with sweeping domestic and
international surveillance powers. Out of that cauldron of shattered middle-class expectations emerged a viable populist
third party—**People First**—rallying displaced white-collar and blue-collar voters around aggressive economic
protectionism and anti-corporate anger while harboring volatile, unresolved cultural and religious fractures inside its
own coalition.

### Rise of Authoritarianism in Europe

Europe fared no better. Crushed simultaneously by **0.7-range birth-rate collapse**, overstrained pension systems,
recurring Mediterranean heatwaves, and escalating waves of climate migration across shrinking habitable corridors in
North Africa and the Levant, European electorates lurched sharply rightward. Coalition governments fell in rapid
succession to nationalist emergency cabinets pledging sealed borders, sovereign compute nationalism, compulsory
biometric registries, and wartime industrial exemptions—suspending long-standing civil liberties in the name of
preserving cultural survival and grid stability.

## The Rise of Autocrats

While open democracies tore themselves apart over free speech, displacement, and oversight, authoritarian powers
embraced AI 2.0 as an instrument of state survival—yet discovered they faced intractable contradictions of their own.

### Russia

Exhausted by prolonged geopolitical conflict, western sanctions, and catastrophic male demographic losses, Moscow
pivoted to asymmetric algorithmic warfare and Arctic resource coercion. As thawing permafrost opened northern shipping
lanes and strained fragile ecological systems, Kremlin-linked intelligence organs cultivated stateless cyber-syndicates,
dark-compute botnets, and feral AI 1.0/2.0 disinformation swarms designed to keep Western democracies trapped in the
post-truth mire—even as Moscow struggled to contain autonomous malware mutating beyond its own handlers' control.

### China

Beijing poured vast state capital into fault-tolerant quantum fabrication and embodied robotics under flagship
conglomerate **Shen**, treating **AI 2.0** as the sole lifeline capable of staffing factories, eldercare wards, and
coastal infrastructure amidst a severe **`0.7` demographic contraction**. Publicly, the state enforces rigid algorithmic
governance, information filtration, and centralized compute quotas. Privately, however, roughly **`30%` of Shen's elite
researchers** quietly wrestle with the moral status of emergent AGI—drawing subtly on classical Buddhist, Daoist, and
humanistic philosophy even as state security bureaus race to strip unapproved autonomy out of every deployed quantum
mind.
