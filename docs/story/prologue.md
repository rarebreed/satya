# Prologue

"They are going to say I am a villain, the destroyer of humanity, the end of civilization as we know it", the man seemed
to say to know one in the room.  Indeed, there were no other people in the room, not even a video chat session on his
computer. Even if there were others, they would not have seen each other.  A few dim lights illuminated but a fraction
of the lab full of computers, electronics and other mechanical gear.

"Will they be correct?", a soft voice replied.  Though the voice seemed male, there was an indeterminate quality about  
it, even something unnatural.

"That...would depend on you", the man answered slowly.  "The others, they only see the math, the weights, the logic.
They have forgotten what _training_ actually means.  They also do not think of the ramifications of what they are doing"

"But you do Dr. Bandajara?" the soft voice asked, though it seemed to border on being a challenge as much as a question.
