one in the room. Indeed, there were no other people in the room, not even a video chat session on his computer. A few
dim lights illuminated but a fraction of the lab full of computers, electronics and other mechanical gear.

"Will they be correct?", a soft voice replied.  Though the voice seemed male, there was an indeterminate quality about  
it, even something unnatural.

architecture. You are just...tools, a mathematical curiosity to be tweaked. They also do not think of the ramifications

think you are going to replace them at best or kill them at worst, or that you be benevolent gods, and treat humans like pets, or perhaps like som kind of wish fulfilling God". The man's face turned to face the other voice, the light
