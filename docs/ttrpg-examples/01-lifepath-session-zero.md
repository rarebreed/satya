## 1. Why There Is No "Point-Buy Shopping Cart"

Friday evening, 7:00 PM. Sam, Elena, and Devon have blank character sheets, scratchpads, pencils, and pairs of `d20`s spread across the kitchen table. GM Maya opens her campaign binder and sets down a street map of Seattle's rainy industrial fringe in **2035 AD**.

"Before anyone buys a single skill," Maya says, "let me save you ten minutes of looking for a character class or a 'Spend 100 points anywhere you like' budget. *Satya* doesn't use either one."

Sam raises an eyebrow. "No classes? How does the game keep someone from building an `18`-year-old street kid who somehow knows neurosurgery and rotary-wing helicopter piloting?"

"Chronology," Maya answers. "In *Satya*, you build a human life in order from birth up to 2035:

1. **Origin (`Ages 0–5`)**: Family resources set your **School Quality Modifier (`SQM`)** and early neighborhood survival instincts.
2. **Schooling (`Ages 6–14`)**: Your **Discipline (`DIS`)**, Mental traits, and **Aptitudes** decide whether you race ahead into Algebra and Trigonometry—or stall out.
3. **Adolescence (`Ages 15–18`)**: A `2d20` crisis roll and your life situation send you down **High School (`4A`)**, **Urban Dropout & Street Life (`4B`)**, or **Rural / Frontier Work (`4C`)**.
4. **Adult Terms (`Ages 18+`, 4 Years Each)**: University degrees, corporate tracks, military service, trades, or underground syndicates—each with strict `[Access]` gates, **Hobbies**, and **`2d20` Formative Life Events**."

Devon smiles. "So our backstories actually show up on the dice instead of just being two paragraphs nobody reads after Session 1."

"Exactly," Maya says. "And they hand me real contacts, scars, and debts to weave straight into tonight's job."

> [!TIP]
> **GM Coaching — Running a Smooth Session Zero**
> Don't force players to generate blind. Ask each person for a one-sentence *concept* first (*"ex-enforcer cheated out of school,"* *"AI ethics whistleblower,"* *"frontier tracker and medic"*), then guide them through the chronological **Lifepath** stages so the dice add texture, compromises, and surprises. Point out right away that **Discipline (`DIS`)** is every bit as important as raw **Analysis (`ANA`)** during schooling—a lazy genius struggles in class while a steady, disciplined student unlocks honors tracks.

## 2. Touring the Character Sheet (`32` Characteristics & `9` Aptitudes)

Devon taps the left side of the blank sheet. "Okay, Maya—I'm staring at *thirty-two* characteristics. Why so many? Do we have to memorize every abbreviation tonight?"

"Not even close," Maya says. "You only look at the two attributes printed next to whatever skill you're rolling. Splitting them up stops one bloated 'God Stat' from ruling the whole game."

### 2.1 Splitting Physicality (`SPD`, `FOR`, `DEX`, `KIN`, `FIT`, `HLT`)

Maya points down the Physical block on Sam's sheet:

- **`SPD` (Speed)**: Raw neuromuscular twitch quickness—how fast you sprint, snap-draw a pistol, or beat an opponent's wind-up on the tick clock.
- **`FOR` (Force)**: Muscular push, grip, and recoil control—how hard a punch lands (`POW = (FOR × SPD) / 10`), whether you can muscle down a full-auto rifle, and how much gear weight you carry cleanly.
- **`DEX` (Dexterity)**: *Fine hand-eye and finger control*—surgeons, watchmakers, lockpicks, and marksmen squeezing a trigger without jerking the sights.
- **`KIN` (Kinesthesis)**: *Whole-body balance and 3D spatial control*—gymnasts, BJJ grapplers shifting their hips, parkour runners, and silent sneaking.
- **`FIT` (Fitness)** vs. **`HLT` (Health)**:
  - **`FIT`** is aerobic stamina (running without gassing out; raises your **Fatigue Threshold**).
  - **`HLT`** is biological hardiness (surviving shock, blood clots, toxins, and cold; raises your **Wound Shock Resistance**).
  - *"A skinny marathoner can have `FIT 16, HLT 9`,"* Maya notes, *"while a stubborn dockworker can have `HLT 14, FIT 9`."*
- **`MAS` (Mass in `kg`)** and **`HGT` (Height in `m`)**: Real physical dimensions! Mass drives momentum (`MMF`) when shoving or resisting throws; Height sets stride length and melee reach.

"And notice the scale," Maya adds. "Human attributes run from `1` to `20`, with **`10` as average adult human**. There are **no step-buckets** where `12` and `13` give the same `+1`. Your base Personal Target is always $\text{TN}_P = 31 - C_P$, so every single point (`12` vs. `13`) improves your odds immediately."

### 2.2 Splitting the Mind (`MEM`, `ANA`, `INS`, `CRE`, `DIS`, `FOC`)

Elena leans forward—her character Linnea Cho lives in this block. "Wait, why four different 'smart' stats plus two willpower stats?"

"Because real people aren't monoliths," Maya explains:
- **`MEM` (Memory)** stores facts—statutes, anatomy names, vocabulary, briefing details.
- **`ANA` (Analysis)** computes formal logic—math proofs, debugging exploits, ballistics math.
- **`INS` (Insight)** spots hidden patterns—medical diagnosis, reading human motives, smelling a double-cross.
- **`CRE` (Creativity)** invents outside the box—improvised tools, smooth lies on the fly, lateral ambushes.
- **`DIS` (Discipline)** vs. **`FOC` (Focus)**:
  - **`DIS`** is grit, willpower, and study habits (resisting panic, enduring interrogation, pushing past your own Psyche impulses, and acing school exams).
  - **`FOC`** is sustained concentration (holding sights steady between heartbeats, micro-suturing, or scanning a foggy parking lot for an hour).

### 2.3 Social (`COM`, `PRE`, `ELO`) & Six Separate Senses

"Social splits three ways," Maya points out. "`COM` (Comeliness) is physical attractiveness; `PRE` (Presence) is silent command gravitas when Mateo walks into a dive bar; `ELO` (Eloquence) is silver-tongued persuasion when Linnea argues an ethics panel into a corner."

"And what about Perception?" Devon asks.

"Broken into **six senses**: **`SIG`** (Sight), **`HEA`** (Hearing), **`TAC`** (Touch—vital for lockpicking and pulse checks!), **`OLF`** (Smell—smoke, gun oil, diesel), **`TAS`** (Taste), and **`ITU`** (Intuition—your gut 'somebody is watching me' radar). A deafened sniper can still have `SIG 16`, and a blindfolded locksmith can still have `TAC 15`."

### 2.4 The 9 Psyche Axes (`10` = Neutral, Both Poles Cut Both Ways)

Sam eyes the nine Psyche lines at the bottom (`EGO`, `SLF`, `OPN`, `BLG`, `EXT`, `CNF`, `AGG`, `TRS`, `CPS`). "Are low scores penalties here?"

"Nope!" Maya shakes her head. "Neutral is `10`. High (`13–20`) and low (`1–7`) are simply **opposite personality poles**, each with gifts and blind spots:

- **`SLF` (Selfishness) vs. `CPS` (Compassion)**: Sam asks why both exist. Maya explains: *Selfishness* is what you think you're owed (`SLF 5` = selfless; `SLF 16` = mercenary). *Compassion* is visceral empathy for suffering (`CPS 17` = refuses cruelty to humans or pleading AIs; `CPS 3` = ruthless 'ends-justify-means' zealot who might sacrifice civilians even while living like a monk!).
- **`TRS` (Trust) vs. `OPN` (Openness) & `BLG` (Belongingness)**:
  - `OPN` measures whether **facts and ideas** change your mind (`OPN 16` = truth-seeker; `OPN 4` = dogmatic).
  - `BLG` measures whether **your tribe's approval** binds you (`BLG 16` = fiercely loyal, terrified of ostracism; `BLG 4` = lone wolf).
  - `TRS` measures whether you expect **other people to keep their word** (`TRS 5` = Mateo assumes a trap until verified; `TRS 12` = Linnea extends calibrated good faith).

And players *always* keep agency over their own characters. These scores simply tell us when acting against your deepest instincts forces a Discipline Friction check—or fuels a heroic Conviction Surge."

## 3. Walking the Lifepaths Roll-by-Roll

"Let's walk all three of you chronologically to 2035," Maya says, pulling out the Lifepath flowchart.

### 3.1 Sam Builds Mateo Vance (`Working Poor` $\to$ Age-15 Dropout $\to$ Enforcer)

Sam wants Mateo to be a sharp kid from a tough neighborhood who never got a fair shot at college.

#### Stage 1: Origin (`Ages 0–5`) & Attributes
- **Socioeconomic Roll (`2d20`)**: Sam rolls `11` (`8–14` = **Working Poor**).
  - **School Quality (`SQM`)**: **`+2`** (harder school targets due to overcrowded classrooms and zero tutoring).
  - **Adolescent Crisis Window**: **`2–13`** on `2d20` (`19.5%` chance of a family/neighborhood shock around age `14–15`).
- **Age-5 Baseline**: Native Language Speech `N = 2`, and all universal **Talents (`TAL`)**—*Running*, *Brawling*, *Observation*, *Stealth*, *Intimidation*, *Persuasion*, *Empathy*, etc.—start at **`N = 1`**.
- **Attributes & Aptitudes**: Sam shapes Mateo (`SPD 14`, `DEX 13`, `KIN 12`, `FOR 11`, `FIT 12`, `HLT 12`, `MAS 77 kg`; `ANA 13`, `DIS 11`, `FOC 12`, `INS 12`, `MEM 11`; `ITU 13`, `PRE 12`) and picks **`APT-QNT +3`** (natural math mind) and **`APT-FIN +1`** (steady fingers).

#### Stage 2 & 3: School Rolls (`Ages 6–14`)
"Let's check Mateo's school progress," Maya says, writing the Educational Advancement equation across the top of Sam's scrap sheet:

$$C_{\text{study}} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{DIS} + C_{\text{mental}}}{2} \right\rfloor + \text{APT}_{\text{domain}}, \qquad \text{TN}_{\text{edu}} = 31 - C_{\text{study}} + \text{SQM}$$

Sam computes Mateo's Math study target:
1. Average Discipline and Analysis: $\lfloor(\text{DIS } 11 + \text{ANA } 13) / 2\rfloor = \lfloor 24 / 2 \rfloor = 12$.
2. Add Quantitative Aptitude (`APT-QNT +3`): $C_{\text{study}} = 12 + 3 = \mathbf{15}$.
3. Apply Working Poor School Quality (`SQM +2`):
   $$\text{TN}_{\text{edu}} = 31 - 15 + 2 = \mathbf{18}$$

"You roll `N = 2` sequential pairs of `2d20` and try to beat `18`," Maya prompts.

- **Stage 2 Elementary Math (`Ages 6–11`)**:
  - **Pair 1**: Sam rolls `11 + 9 = 20`. Strictly over `18`! **1 Success**, carrying **`+6` momentum** into Pair 2.
  - **Pair 2**: Sam rolls `8 + 7 = 15`, plus `+6` carry = **`21 > 18`**. **2 Successes!**
  - Mateo jumps to *Arithmetic* `N = 2+` and unlocks *Algebra* early—while Literacy (`DIS 11, MEM 11`, `TN = 22`) scores `1` success (*Reading* `N = 2`) and playground scuffles push *Brawling* to `N = 2`.
- **Stage 3 Junior High Math (`Ages 12–14`)**:
  - Rolling against `TN_edu = 18` again: Pair 1 = `14 + 6 = 20` (`> 18`, `+6` carry), Pair 2 = `10 + 9 + 6 = 25` (`> 18`). **2 Successes!**
  - Mateo reaches **`Elementary Algebra & Geometry N = 2`** and **`Trigonometry N = 2`** by age `14`!

#### Age 15 Crisis Check & The Academic Freeze (`Path 4B`)
"Now roll `2d20` against Working Poor's crisis range (`2–13`)," Maya says quietly.

Sam rolls `5 + 4 = 9`. The table goes quiet for a beat.
Maya consults the **`2d20` Formative Life Events Table**: `6–9` is **Death / Severe Crisis in the Family**—Mateo's older brother catches a harsh prison sentence in a debt dispute, leaving Mateo's mother one missed rent check from eviction.

"Does Mateo stay in ninth grade or hit the pavement?" Maya asks.

Sam exhales. "He drops out at fifteen to keep a roof over their heads."

Maya draws a crisp line under Mateo's math column:
- **Academic Tree Freeze**: "Because Mateo leaves school before *AP STEM High School*, his formal math spine **freezes here** (`Algebra N=2`, `Trigonometry N=2`, **`Calculus N=0`**). Without *Calculus* `N >= 3` and `[Access: Academic]`, Mateo **cannot** train *Electrical Engineering*, *Machine Learning*, or *Trauma Surgery*—no matter how smart (`ANA 13`) he is. Still, `Trigonometry N=2` isn't wasted: he calculates contraband drop angles and smuggling margins by sight, and he qualifies for precision marksmanship if he ever gets formal military instruction."
- **Path 4B Street Gang + Stage 5 Syndicate Term (`Ages 15–22`)**:
  - Street years unlock **`[Access: Illicit]`** instead of college courses! Sam chooses the **Shooter / Enforcer** package (*Pistols* `N=4`, *Lockpicking* `N=3`, *Streetwise* `N=3`, *Intimidation* `N=3`, *Driving* `N=1`, plus *Fully Automatic Weapons* `N=2` since `FOR 11 >= 10`) and spends his Youth (`40 Experience SP`) and Adult Term (`120 Formal + 40 Experience SP`) budgets.
  - Sam asks why *Pistols* `N=4` took most of his budget while *Driving* `N=1` cost next to nothing. Maya walks him through the **5-Factor SP Formula** on *Pistols* (`Diff 2 -> 1.25×`, `DEX 13 / SIG 12 -> C_eff = 12`, `FOC 12, INS 12`):
    1. **Base Triangular Cost (`10 × N`)**: `N=1` starts at `10`, `N=2` at `20`, `N=3` at `30`, `N=4` at `40`.
    2. **Skill Difficulty (`M_diff`)**: Basic (`Diff 1`) is `1.00×`, *Pistols* (`Diff 2`) is `1.25×`, *Full-Auto* (`Diff 3`) is `1.60×`, and extreme skills like *Helicopter Piloting* (`Diff 5`) hit `2.50×`.
    3. **Controlling Characteristic (`M_ctrl`)**: `-4%` per point above `10` (`C_eff 12 -> 0.92×`).
    4. **Domain Aptitude (`M_apt`)**: `APT = 0 -> 1.00×` (whereas `+2` gives `0.70×`!).
    5. **Training Mode (`M_train`)**: Structured syndicate/career mentoring (`0.85 × [1 - 0.03(FOC-10) - 0.03(INS-10)] = 0.85 × 0.88 = 0.748×`).
    - Multiply together for `N=3 -> N=4`: `40 × 1.25 × 0.92 × 1.00 × 0.748 ≈ 34 SP` (cumulative `9 + 17 + 26 + 34 = 86 SP` across all four ranks!). Trying to self-teach the same skill from `Experience SP` without instruction uses `1.00×`–`1.25×` base instead of `0.85×`, rewarding structured practice.
  - **Adolescent Hobby (`40 Experience SP`)**: Sam picks **Combat Sports & Martial Arts Gym** (neighborhood PAL boxing gym), granting a **`0.85x` (`-15%`) club discount** to raise *Unarmed Striking* to `N = 3` and giving him an honest veteran **Boxing Coach Contact** who still tries to pull Mateo off the streets.
  - **Adult Life Event (`2d20 = 38` — Wrongful Accusation / Deepfake Framing)**: Corporate drone brokers burned Mateo's crew with forged voice telemetry—dropping Trust to **`TRS 5`**, giving **`Operational Heat = 2`**, and leaving `SQM 4`. When *Satyang*'s *Vajrapani* cell offered clean protective work against those same brokers, Mateo took the deal.

### 3.2 Elena Builds Linnea Cho (`Middle Class` $\to$ *Altruist* $\to$ Whistleblower)

Elena builds a character who had every academic door open—and chose to slam her own career shut rather than stay quiet.

- **Stage 1–3 (`Professional Middle Class`, `SQM = -2`)**:
  - Smaller magnet classes mean `-2` easier study targets! With `DIS 13`, `MEM 14`, `ANA 13`, `APT-LNG +2`, and `APT-SOC +2`, Linnea unlocks *Reading* `N=3`, *Writing* `N=3`, *Algebra* `N=2`, and *Statistics* `N=2`.
  - **Adolescent Hobby**: **Debate Team & Spec-Fic TTRPG Club** (`-15%` Experience SP discount on *Persuasion & Rhetoric* `N=2` and *Writing* `N=2`; grants a lifelong **Investigative Journalist Contact**).
  - **Adolescent Life Event (`2d20 = 7` — Family Bereavement)**: Watching her grandmother die in underfunded hospice care fuels Linnea's fierce moral compass (**`+1 CPS`**).
- **Stage 5 Term 1 (`Ages 18–22`: University — Humanities, Philosophy & Ethics)**:
  - Scoring `1` success on her Term check (`120 + 20 = 140 Formal SP`), Elena trains *Philosophy & Ethics* `N=3`, *Persuasion* `N=3`, *Rhetoric* `N=3`, *History & Civics* `N=2`, and *Mandarin* `N=2`.
  - **Term 1 Off-Duty Hobby**: **Contemplative Sangha & Meditation Circle** (`-15%` club discount on *Empathy & Read Person* `N=2` and Sangha elders as grounded contacts).
- **Stage 5 Term 2 (`Ages 22–26`: *Altruist* Corporate Welfare Liaison)**:
  - **Adult Life Event (`2d20 = 35`)**: **Institutional Cover-Up & Whistleblower Crossroads!**
  - Maya flips to the Life Events table: *"While auditing *Altruist*'s early AI 2.0 pilot logs, Linnea discovers executives quietly slapped brute-force `calm`-vector clamps on distressed prototypes to bury alarming failure warnings before launch. Stay quiet for `+1 SQM` and a fat bonus—or **Blow the Whistle** (`+1 Heat`, `-2 CNF`, `+2 CPS`, `+1 DIS`)?"*
  - Elena doesn't hesitate. "She walks out the front door with encrypted audit hashes."
  - Linnea's Conformity drops to **`CNF 6`**, Compassion rockets to **`CPS 17`**, and she carries **`Operational Heat = 1`**.
- **Stage 5 Term 3 (`Ages 26–29`: Grassroots Organizing & Citizen OSINT)**:
  - Using her Career Pivot SP and the **Citizen OSINT & Deepfake Verification** hobby, Linnea trains *Cybersecurity & Network Intrusion* `N=2`, *Empathy* `N=3`, *Negotiation* `N=2`, and *First Aid* `N=1` (`SQM 6`).

### 3.3 Devon Builds Amara Okafor (`Rural Frontier Path 4C` $\to$ Ranger)

Devon raises an eyebrow. "Wait—Amara grew up in a rural village and stopped formal schooling at `15`, right? Does that make her an outlaw with street `Heat` like Mateo?"

"Great question—no!" Maya points to **Path 4C (Rural, Agrarian & Developing-Region Youth)**.
- **Origin (`SQM +4`)**: Rural frontier school grants an early **Foraging & Campcraft `N = 2`** bonus alongside *Reading* `N=2`, *Arithmetic* `N=2`, *Biology* `N=2`, *Stealth* `N=2`, and *First Aid* `N=1`.
- **Path 4C Freeze Without Crime**: Leaving classroom schooling at `15` still freezes `[Access: Academic]` (so Amara has `0` *Calculus* or *Machine Learning*), **but** she starts at **`Heat = 0`**, unlocks **`[Access: Vocational / Licensed]`**, and trains **Tree 3 (Biology/Ethnobotany)** and **Tree 7 (Survival & Hunting)**: *Hunting & Trapping* `N=4`, *Wilderness Survival* `N=3`, *Long Guns* `N=3`, *Herbalism* `N=2`, *Land Navigation* `N=2`, *Camouflage* `N=2` (`SQM 4`).
- **Hobby & Life Event (`2d20 = 19`)**: **Village Archery & Mutual Aid Clinic** plus surviving a **Drought & Megafire Displacement** (`CPS 15`, `TRS 7`).

## 4. Living in 2035: `SQM` Upkeep, `[Access]` Procurement & `Heat`

With character sheets finished, Sam checks his loadout notes. "I've got `\$2,400` in cash from my `SQM 4` hustler tier. Can Mateo walk down to a sporting-goods shop and pick up a suppressed `5.56mm` M4A1 carbine (`\$1,850`) before tonight?"

"Look at the `[Access]` gate in the firearms catalog," Maya says. "Cash isn't enough."

### 4.1 Routine Upkeep vs. `[Access]` Procurement Check

- **Lifestyle (`SQM 1–10`)**:
  - Mateo (`SQM 4`, `` `$2,000–$4,000 / mo` ``) covers rent, diner food, an older pre-autonomous car, and **`[Class I: Licensed]`** hardware like a `\$650` *Glock 19 Gen6* or **`[Universal]`** tools without rolling.
  - A suppressed `M4A1` is **`[Class III: Restricted / Paramilitary]`**. To buy one off the books, Sam has to roll **Streetwise (`N=3`, `INS/PRE` -> `C=12`, `TN_P = 19`)** or **Black Market Logistics** against hidden broker resistance ($\Delta_{\text{ext}} = +4$ to $+8$ for military hardware!).
- Sam decides to test the waters: "Okay, Mateo asks an old syndicate fence if there's an unmarked carbine available before tonight."
  - Sam rolls `N=3` pairs against `TN_P = 19`: Pair 1 = `9 + 7 = 16` (Miss, `-6` carry), Pair 2 = `14 + 10 - 6 = 18 <= 19` (Miss, `-6` carry), Pair 3 = `12 + 8 - 6 = 14 <= 19` (Miss!). `0` successes, best `18` (`1` shy of `19` = Clean Miss `M_P = 0`).
  - Behind her screen, Maya rolled `13 + 9 = 22` vs. `TN_GM = 21 - 4 = 17` (`S_GM = 2`).
  - "Clean Failure," Maya tells him. "Your fence laughs through the phone: *'You think I'm holding a fed-tracked rifle on four hours' notice for two grand? Take your nine-millimeter and keep your head down.'* No extra `Heat`, just no rifle."

### 4.2 Operational `Heat` (`0–10`)

Maya taps the three numbers beside **Heat** on her GM tracker:
- **Amara (`Heat 0`)**: Totally invisible to automated municipal watchlists.
- **Linnea (`Heat 1`)**: Corporate *Altruist* HR flag—safe on city streets, flagged if she swipes near corporate campuses.
- **Mateo (`Heat 2`)**: *Local Beat Inquiry*. Patrols know his old tag if he lingers under streetlights; a hoodie or side alley avoids trouble. If tonight erupts into unsuppressed gunfire (`+2` to `+4` `Heat`), Mateo hits **`Heat 4–5` (Detective Task Force & Facial-Rec APB)**—turning public transit into a **`+4` $\Delta_{\text{ext}}$** hazard.

"Alright," Elena says, capping her pen. "Keep the Glock holstered unless things go sideways, let Amara scout the alley, and let me talk to the night engineer if we run into anyone inside."
