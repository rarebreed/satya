## 1. Core Genre & Visual Identity: *Pre-Apocalyptic Threshold Realism*

The visual tone of *Satya: Awakened* blends **Noir**, **Espionage / Covert Ops**, **Investigative Mystery**, and **Pre-Apocalyptic Socio-Ecological Dread**—the exact tension captured by *Deus Ex: Human Revolution*: *"It isn't the end of the world, but you can see it from here."*

Everything should feel like an unsettlingly plausible **2035 photograph from our own near future**: lived-in, dusty, under-maintained municipal infrastructure pressed right up against razor-wired frontier compute campuses, while ordinary, tired human beings confront impossible moral and political fractures.

### Strict Genre Guardrails (DO / DO NOT)

| DO (Canonical 2035 Realism) | DO NOT (Rejected Clichés & Continuity Errors) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Matte-black **2030s flat-panel / ultrawide monitors**, slim keyboards, pre-2026 air-gapped ThinkPads, ruggedized field telemetry **Pads** | Retro 1980s/1990s beige CRT monitors, floating holographic UIs, glowing VR cyberspace grids, or big brass Frankenstein knife-switches |
| Sprawling **low windowless precast-concrete datacenter chiller halls** with **roof-mounted evaporative louver arrays** | Tall hyperbolic **nuclear-plant cooling towers** masquerading as AI datacenters |
| Completely **dry, condensation-free** cryo tanks, stainless helium manifolds, and matte-black `SSM`/server racks | **Ice, frost, or icicles** coating electronics or server racks (cryo stays sealed inside stainless dilution-refrigerator cans) |
| **Non-glowing matte dark optical camera irises** on humanoids, quadrupeds, and UAVs; subtle amber/green pinhead telemetry LEDs at most | Glowing red, cyan, or white Terminator-style robot eyes; over-bulky sci-fi Warhammer/mech silhouettes |
| Everyday human clothing, scuffed drywall, `500 kV` substation transformers, copper Faraday mesh, CAN-bus spoofers, HAM radios, stripped manual getaway hatchbacks | Cyberpunk neon magenta/cyan cityscapes, chrome cyber-arms, flying cars, post-apocalyptic nuclear rubble, or spotless utopian sci-fi corridors |
| **Diverse weather, seasons & times of day**: humid summer pre-storms, golden-hour arid plains, crisp dry dusk, cool overcast morning interiors | Every scene looking like dark rain-slicked midnight asphalt (*rainy night is reserved selectively, not defaulted*) |
| **Greenish-grey optical military camo** tactical uniforms on DAISE paramilitary squads/marshals with visors or face masks | Black sci-fi armored riot troopers or futuristic halo-visor space marines |
| **Zero overlaid text**: bare artwork edges; allow *only* authentic diegetic signage cleanly anchored on physical banners, vest tags, or doors when scripted | Baked-in illustration **title banners, captions, borders, watermarks**, or putting `"titled ..."` inside image prompts |

## 2. The Dual House Styles

Early style testing retired overly loose painterly fantasy brushwork in favor of **two complementary house styles** that share grounded architectural/hardware proportions while serving different editorial roles:

### Style B — Suburban & Industrial Pastoral (*Simon Stålenhag Influence*)
- **Visual Recipe**: Broad, tactile atmospheric brushwork across skies, haze, mist, distant tree lines, and shadow washes paired with razor-crisp, weathered mechanical and suburban realism in foreground objects. Understated melancholy; ordinary architecture sitting uncomfortably beside massive AI infrastructure, dry riverbeds, or quiet robotics benches.
- **Lighting & Palette**: Naturalistic twilight, pale cool-morning daylight, humid late-afternoon summer heat-haze, amber sodium streetlamps against dusty olive-grey and faded concrete tones.
- **Best Used For**:
  - Wide world-building & geopolitical/ecological double spreads
  - Quiet uncanny stillness (synthetic minds, cryo laboratories, deserted automated workplaces)
  - Hardware stills, analog table scenes, and contemplative sanctuaries
- **Canonical Reference Plates**:
  - [01_asymmetric_grid_20c_behind_the_wire.jpg](assets/01_asymmetric_grid_20c_behind_the_wire.jpg) *(wide world contrast, precast datacenter chiller halls)*
  - [11_slime_mold_edge_summer_patrol.jpg](assets/11_slime_mold_edge_summer_patrol.jpg) *(dry summer pre-storm daylight & greenish-grey optical camo patrol)*
  - [19_chan_monastery_four_bodhisattvas.jpg](assets/19_chan_monastery_four_bodhisattvas.jpg) *(darker candlelit interior chiaroscuro, intimate monk scale, active statues)*
  - [20_luzon_pax_silica_sacrifice_zone.jpg](assets/20_luzon_pax_silica_sacrifice_zone.jpg) *(tropical heat-haze double spread, trike/carabao sacrifice zone)*
  - [04_alley_barter_echo9.jpg](assets/04_alley_barter_echo9.jpg) *(moody urban hardware stillness)*
  - [09_cryogenic_quantum_chandelier.jpg](assets/09_cryogenic_quantum_chandelier.jpg) *(clean technical equipment realism)*
  - [18_uncanny_valley_mimic.jpg](assets/18_uncanny_valley_mimic.jpg) *(quiet synthetic portraiture)*

### Style C — Painted Cel-Shaded 3D (*Disney+ "What If...?" Influence*)
- **Visual Recipe**: Crisp sculpted 3D anatomy and prop geometry surfaced with **directional hand-painted brushstroke textures**, **bold angular black ink contour lines**, and **hard stepped cel-shadow terminators** (never muddy soft airbrushing or flat uniform ambient lighting).
- **Lighting & Palette**: High-contrast noir two-tone rim lighting—warm sodium amber, sunbeam shafts, flashlight beams, lightning glow, or vehicle brake-red slicing sharply across deep slate-teal or indigo shadow planes.
- **Best Used For**:
  - Character confrontations, whistleblowers, and ensemble espionage briefings
  - Social Combat standoffs (`TRS`/`CPS` wedges, pickets, civic plazas)
  - Kinetic `0.25s` tactical sequences (melee traps, lockpicking, corridor stealth, vehicular chases) and crisp weapon plates
- **Canonical Reference Plates**:
  - [03_warrant_at_st_jude_annex.jpg](assets/03_warrant_at_st_jude_annex.jpg) *(flagship Style C interior standoff)*
  - [12_harlan_reeves_picket_line.jpg](assets/12_harlan_reeves_picket_line.jpg) *(outdoor crowd & character portraiture)*
  - [21_moral_horizon_arc3_convergence.jpg](assets/21_moral_horizon_arc3_convergence.jpg) *(twilight city & internally lit cumulonimbus thunder cell)*

  - [17_embodied_humanoid_robots.jpg](assets/17_embodied_humanoid_robots.jpg) *(hard-surface robotics in crisp Style C ink/cel-shading)*

## 3. Print Layout Slots & Supported Aspect Ratios

All illustrations are composed cleanly around printed TTRPG book geometry:

| Book Layout Slot | Target Aspect Ratio | Composition Rules |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Double-Page Spread** | `3:2` Landscape | **Strict Gutter-Safe Rule**: Zero human faces, key badges, or focal silhouettes inside the center 10% spine zone. Route aisles, roads, canals, concrete dividers, or empty architectural gaps cleanly through the vertical centerline. |
| **Full-Page Plate** | `3:4` Portrait | Note: Use `3:4` rather than unsupported `4:5`. Leave breathable negative space (sky, brick/concrete wall, rafters) in the upper third when framing chapter openers. |
| **Half-Page Banner** | `16:9` or `3:2` Landscape | Tight horizontal storytelling or two-subject hardware comparison. |
| **Half-Page Column** | `9:16` Vertical | Steep vertical architecture (stairwells, corridors, canyon sightlines). |
| **Quarter-Page Spot** | `1:1`, `4:3`, or `3:4` | Focused 1–2 subject vignette, still life, or intimate character threshold. |

## 4. Canonical Character, Faction & Hardware Continuity

Maintain consistent physical appearance across plates:

- **Dr. Budi Bandajara**: **Half-Malaysian** (Eurasian: Chinese-Malay mother & Irish-Canadian father; slightly more Southeast Asian facial features), early 40s (`42`), `180 cm` mesomorphic frame, dark hair heavily greying/white at the temples, hooded eyes, square jawline, dark-rimmed glasses, rumpled tan-brown coat over dark collared shirt; carries an analog paper notebook and interacts with a modern ruggedized telemetry **Pad** rather than analog switches or smart-glass mics.
- **Alex Wen**: Mid-20s (`25`), three-quarters Chinese and one-quarter Malay (Dr. Bandajara's nephew), `178 cm` athletic build, warm Southeast Asian complexion, short tousled jet-black hair; olive-grey t-shirt, jeans, brown boots, or olive work jacket with medical duffel (`alex_wen_set01` hero reference).
- **Mei-Ling "Lotus" Lin**: Chinese-American community clinic organizer, worn knit cardigan, St. Jude Annex clinic lanyard, composed protective stance.
- **Agent Marcus Chen (DAISE)**: East Asian federal investigator (`37`), unmarked dark navy windbreaker, visible gold DAISE federal belt badge, ruggedized tablet.
- **Agent Dev Blake (DAISE)**: Imposing broad-shouldered African-American federal investigator (`~192 cm`, Marcus Chen's partner), close-cropped dark hair, neat short beard, calm heavy-lidded watchful eyes; matte charcoal field jacket over dark crewneck henley, cargo trousers, gold DAISE federal belt badge.
- **Kate Springer**: Age `19` Caucasian young woman, slightly messy shoulder-length light brown/auburn hair tucked behind one ear, oversized faded slate-blue thrift hoodie, subtle silver chain, soft tired expressive eyes; carries a worn canvas backpack when running quiet twilight errands.
- **Cliff Reed (*Translucent*)**: Late 30s Caucasian AI scientist, sharp angular features, neatly styled sandy-brown hair, frameless rectangular glasses, confident scoffing half-smile; fitted charcoal merino crewneck over white collared shirt, corporate ID lanyard, slim matte-black tablet.
- **Jose Dominguez (*Altruist*)**: Early 40s Hispanic/Latino ex-Secret Service cyber & close-protection lead, compact muscular build, military-neat short dark hair, close-trimmed salt-and-pepper beard, discreet clear coiled acoustic earpiece; tailored matte dark-slate blazer over black crewneck, minimalist security lapel pin.
- **Harlan Reeves (*People First* / *Humanitas Prima*)**: Broad-shouldered working-class union foreman, soaked/weathered tan canvas work jacket over dark hoodie, union cap, bullhorn.
- **Amara Okafor (*Frontier Ranger* PC)**: Age 22 Black African bush tracker, dusty olive/earth utility vest over faded long-sleeve shirt, slung bolt-action hunting rifle, binoculars.
- **Linnea Cho (*Manjusri / Avalokitesvara* PC)**: Age 29 East Asian ex-*Altruist* alignment ethicist, understated coat, grappling with corporate badge vs. encrypted air-gapped micro-drive.
- **DAISE Paramilitary Patrols / Federal Marshals**: Dressed in **greenish-grey military optical camo** tactical uniforms, plate carriers, matte ballistic helmets, tinted visors or half-face respirators—never sleek black sci-fi armor.
- **Rank-and-File Minor & "Cannon Fodder" Faction Archetypes**:
  - *Satyang Volunteers & Watch Escorts*: Worn parkas, clinic ID lanyards, analog two-way radios, and dark canvas utility jackets with subtle coiled earpieces—never tactical armor.
  - *Corporate SecOps Contractors*: Matte dark-slate softshell tactical jackets, low-profile plate carriers, coiled earpieces, minimalist contractor ID lanyards, and compact carbines/shotguns; *Datacenter Floor Techs* wear grey-and-amber hi-vis vests, neck-worn hearing protection, and work strictly beside dry, frost-free matte-black `SSM` racks.
  - *People First Picketers & Humanitas Vanguard Saboteurs*: Union caps, flannel shirts, and weathered canvas jackets around burn barrels for picket regulars; olive/charcoal hoodies, half-face respirators, bolt cutters, copper grounding chains, and civilian shotguns for clandestine grid-strike cells.
  - *Rogue AI Gig Proxies & Feral Drones*: Thrifted hoodies, reflective messenger bags, burner smartphones, and scuffed Pelican cases for human cutouts; zip-tied aftermarket Li-ion packs and non-glowing dark optical lenses on scuffed commercial quadrupeds and quadrotors.
- **Chan Buddhist Monks & The Four Bodhisattvas (*Satyang* Roots)**:
  - **Sanctuary Architecture & Lighting**: Dark, intimate, windowless timber hall anchored by **four massive glossy dark-lacquered wooden pillars** (ZERO side windows or daylight shafts from side walls); lit strictly by tall altar candles, floor votive candle trays, and a subtle cool open-door twilight spill creeping across the foreground floorboards from behind the viewer.
  - **Chan Monks Posture & Scale**: Packed tightly shoulder-to-shoulder in compact facing rows (`5` per side across a narrow gutter-safe wooden aisle) at intimate close scale, wearing **muted dark earthy brown and dark ash-grey *haiqing* cotton robes** (NEVER bright orange/saffron Theravada or dark red Tibetan robes), sitting cross-legged on floor meditation mats/cushions while **bowing forward together in synchronized seated prayer (*heshi*)**.
  - **Shrine, Altar & Four Sculpted Statues**: Grand tiered dark-wood rear shrine and offering altar holding a **small seated bronze Buddha statue**, **twin small bronze stupas**, and a smoking bronze incense censer, flanked on carved pedestals by **four three-dimensional sculpted statues** (preferred over flat murals): **Manjusri** (actively leaning forward swinging his wisdom sword as if to cut through ignorance, sutra scroll in left hand), **Avalokitesvara / Guanyin** (serene with willow branch & water vase), **Ksitigarbha / Dizang** (monastic shaved head, six-ringed *khakkhara* staff & wish-fulfilling pearl), and **Vajrapani** (muscular wrathful guardian coiled back ready to hurl his bronze *vajra* thunderbolt).
- **Embodied Robots & Drones**:
  - *Nagaraja (Full-Body Prototype Android)*: `~178 cm` athletic mid-weight humanoid silhouette sitting squarely between slender domestic Unitree G1 / Tesla frames and heavy industrial Atlas frames; sculpted matte warm-graphite and dark-titanium anatomical contours with woven myomer-tendon bands at neck/elbows/knees, expressive human-like face evolved from `#02`, and **twin non-glowing concentric dark optical camera irises** (ZERO glowing eyes).
  - *Industrial Agile Humanoid*: Trim human-scale Boston Dynamics Atlas-like athletic proportions (`~1.7–1.8m`), exposed matte composite/aluminum actuators—never over-bulked Warhammer frames.
  - *Svelte Agile Humanoid*: Compact Unitree G1-style slender articulated limbs.
  - *Uncanny-Valley Mimic*: Lifelike silicone face, **long straight feminine dark hair** tucked behind one ear revealing subtle sub-dermal jaw/neck articulation seam, **non-glowing dark matte optical camera irises**.
  - *Morphology Systems `QPed-590` (Segmented Quadruped)*: 4 articulated matte olive-grey composite body segments flexing along a reinforced dorsal spine for climbing/leaping, multi-pad carbon paws with curved matte-steel traction claws, stereoscopic twin-lens non-glowing bi-camera head, and wrap-around modular dark-grey mounting harness (weapons, comms mast, micro-radar, or cargo panniers).
  - *Expanded Field Drones*: Palm-sized `250 g` `MicroScoutQuadrotor`, `1.8 kg` toroidal-shrouded `TacticalQuadrotorFpv`, `65 kg` armored `TrackedBreacherUgw` with `6-DoF` manipulator arm, `18 kg` coaxial/octo-rotor `HeavyCargoOrWeaponVtol`, six-wheeled UGV cargo mule, and V-tail tactical ISR fixed-wing craft.

## 5. Prompting & Visual QA Checklist

1. **Attach 1–2 canonical reference plates** matching the chosen style (Style B vs. Style C) so palette, line weight, and shader grain stay locked.
2. **State weather, time of day, and hardware era explicitly**: e.g., *"dry summer afternoon heat haze, zero rain"*, *"slim matte-black 2030s flat-panel monitors, zero glowing holograms, completely dry spotless server racks with zero frost"*.
3. **Never put `titled "..."` inside a visual prompt**, and close every prompt with: `"ZERO overlaid text, ZERO title bars, ZERO captions, ZERO borders, ZERO watermarks."`
4. **Inspect every generated image directly** before staging:
   - Verify Style C has visible black ink outlines and hard stepped cel-shadows (not soft airbrush).
   - Verify structural safety/realism (e.g., stairwell guardrails present, correct weapon silhouettes, proper monk robe colors, all 4 Bodhisattvas recognizable).
   - Limit retries to focused single-pass corrections targeting the specific defect observed.
