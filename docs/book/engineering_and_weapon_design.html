Engineering, Workshops & Custom Weapon/Armor Design

Rust Engine Implementation: crates/satya-engine/src/engineering_design.rs (WorkshopFacilityTier, ProjectClockComplexity, PrototypeQuirkId, BulletConstructionKind, build_custom_ammunition_profile, build_custom_firearm_design, evaluate_custom_melee_design, evaluate_custom_armor_design), wrapping crates/satya-engine/src/ballistics.rs (evaluate_granular_firearm_ballistics). Companion Chapters: Extended Project Clocks (cascading_d20_and_tasks.md) · Firearm Engineering & Ballistics (firearms_and_ballistics.md) · Ranged Combat & Armor (ranged_combat_and_armor.md) · Lifepaths, Black Market & Heat (lifepaths_and_economy.md).

In Satya, characters do not fabricate aerospace-grade firearm barrels or NIJ IV ceramic strike plates with a cordless Dremel in a motel room. Custom engineering extends the core Extended Project Clock (cascading_d20_and_tasks.md §6.3) and couples directly into the existing Granular Ballistics engine (ballistics.rs) and Body Armor PR / BABT architecture (ranged_combat_and_armor.md). No separate arbitrary damage system exists—your workshop tier caps your achievable machining tolerance τtol and manufacturing quality Qmfg, and every physical dimension you select flows straight through the equations in Chapter 16.

1. Engineering R&D Pipeline: Workshops, Project Clocks & Quirks

1.1 Workshop Facility Tiers (WorkshopFacilityTier)

Your machining hardware establishes both your known Tool Modifier (Δtool) and hard physical ceilings on machining tolerance (τmax), barrel bore uniformity (Qbbl,max), and metallurgy/coating finish (Qmfg,max). Attempting to build beyond a facility's ceiling clamps the finished hardware to the shop's limit and injects a Prototype Quirk.

1.3 Prototype Quirks (PrototypeQuirkId) & Masterwork Finish

  • Masterwork Bonus (BuildOutcomeQuality::MasterworkExtraordinary): Achieving at least one ExtraordinarySuccess interval with zero unresolved Floor Breaches inside a CommercialCncMachineShop or AerospaceCleanroomLab grants +1 Q_mfg (capped at +2), raising Reliability Rel or Armor BTD by +1.

2. Custom Firearm & Ammunition Designer

Custom firearms assemble six engineering choices (caliber, bullet, action_type, feed_type, barrel_length_cm + barrel_mounting, and muzzle_device + center_of_gravity) and pass the resulting GranularFirearmSpec straight into evaluate_granular_firearm_ballistics(&spec, distance_m) (firearms_and_ballistics.md §3–§6):

ηbbl = clamp(1 + 0.35 ln(LbblLopt),  0.60,  1.15) + Δηmuzzle,    Ek(d) = Echem · ηbbl · ηaction · e−d / Ddecay
Acc = clamp(round(ΔAccaction + ΔAccwt + ΔAccCoG + ΔAcclen + Qbbl + ΔAccmount + τtol + Qammo),  −4,  +4)

4. Worked Example: CNC Suppressed .300 BLK Carbine vs. Garage Improv

AT THE TABLE

Scenario: Amara Okafor wants a custom "Covert Whisper-300 (.300 BLK Sub)" direct-impingement upper (CustomSubassembly = 8 Successes, 22.9 cm free-float barrel, integral SoundSuppressor, 20-rd box mag, MatchOtm 220 gr subsonic handloads, requesting τtol = +0.5, Qbbl = +1.0, Qmfg = +1).

  1. Built in a CommercialCncMachineShop (-2 Δtool) with MasterworkExtraordinary Roll:
    • Facility ceilings (τmax=+1.0, Qbbl,max=+1.0, Qmfg,max=+1) accept her specs un-clamped, and the Extraordinary interval pushes Qmfg = +2 with 0 Quirks.
    • Via evaluate_granular_firearm_ballistics at 50 m: ηbbl = 1.02, ηaction = 0.97, Emuzzle = 686.2 J, Ek(50 m) = 587.0 J ⟹ IKE = 3, Ical = 2, Acc = +4, W_yaw = 0, Raw Impact = 5, Pen = 4, Rel = +1, estimated cost $3,542, Class III (SoundSuppressor).
  2. Built on a FieldBenchImprovised (+4 Δtool) with a FloorBreach:
    • Facility ceilings clamp tolerances to τ = −0.5, Qbbl = −1.0, Qmfg = −1, dropping precision to Acc = +1, Rel = 0, and attaching FinickyAmmoTolerance, ThermalDriftBarrel, and ProprietaryPartsOnly.
