Core 2d20 Watershed Cascade & Always-Contested Task Resolution

Whenever a character performs an action with meaningful stakes and uncertainty that can influence the narrative or tactical situation, the Game Master (or the Satya AI Game Master via ContestedCascadeTaskParams and evaluate_satya_cascade_task in crates/satya-engine/src/dice_probabilities.rs) calls for an Always-Contested Task Resolution roll.

In Satya, every task is contested: the player rolls their sequential 2d20 cascade against their known personal target number (TNP = 31 − CP + Δtool), while the Game Master secretly rolls an opposing GM Resistance Roll (1-Pair Tiered GM Resistance for environmental/technical obstacles against TNGM = 21 − Δext, or a full opposing cascade for an active NPC opponent). Because the GM's target and roll are hidden, the player can feel their own execution momentum and floor-breach strain on the dice, but never knows for certain whether a high roll beat the hidden resistance or whether a 0-success near-miss was rescued by a GM botch until the narrative outcome unfolds.

This chapter is the single canonical ruleset for framing, gating, modifying, and resolving any task or skill check in Satya, uniting the mathematical calibration in Probability, Distribution Topology & 2d20 Calibration and the Mathematical Notation, Variables & Formula Glossary with the attribute controllers in Character Characteristics & Aptitudes and the skill hierarchy in Skill System & The 8 Hierarchical Skill Trees.

2. Step 1: Task Gate Classifications, Prerequisites & Tools

Before any dice are rolled, the GM determines whether the character is qualified and equipped to attempt the task.

2.1 Three Task Gate Classifications (Class A / B / C)

Every task in Satya belongs to one of three Gate Classifications:

2.2 Tool Dependency Tiers ([Tool]) & 2031–2035 Smart-Tech Modifiers

Every skill and task specifies one of three tool dependencies:

  1. [Tool: None]: Relies purely on the character's body, voice, senses, or mind (Running, Singing, Brawling, Observation, Mental Arithmetic, Persuasion, Empathy).
  2. [Tool: Situational]: Unassisted tasks can be performed barehanded (Δext = 0), while complex tasks within the same skill require tools or suffer an Improvised/Unassisted Penalty (Δext = +4 to +12)—e.g., First Aid allows direct pressure barehanded, but requires a trauma kit to tourniquet severed arterial vessels or decompress a tension pneumothorax; Climbing allows scrambling up a brick wall barehanded, but requires pitons/ascenders for a sheer glass skyscraper.
  3. [Tool: Mandatory]: The skill cannot be rolled at all without the requisite weapon, instrument, vehicle, lab, or computing rig (Lockpicking, Trauma Surgery, Network Exploitation, Firearms, Rotary-Wing Piloting, Arc Welding).

3. Step 2: The Four Task Factors (N Skills, Target Number 31 - C, Δint, Δext & Degrees of Success)

Every task in Satya is parameterized by four orthogonal factors (CascadeTaskParams), determining the Roll-Over Target Number (TNeff = 31 − C + Δext) and the resulting Degree of Success (OutcomeTier):

3.1 Factor 1: Skill Level (N ∈ [1, 8], Mythic Cap 10) — Number of 2d20 Pairs

When attempting a skill check or combat action, a character rolls N sequential pairs of 20-sided dice (2d20), where N is the character's Skill Level:

List A = (A1, A2, …, AN),    List B = (B1, B2, …, BN)

At each step n ∈ {1, …, N}, the unmodified pair sum Xn = An + Bn ranges from 2 to 40, forming a triangular distribution centered at its exact midpoint of 21.

3.2 Factor 2: Controlling Characteristic (C ∈ [1, 20]) — Baseline Target Shift

Satya is a Roll-Over system where higher numbers are always better:

  • A higher pair sum (An + Bn) is always better.
  • A higher Controlling Characteristic (C) (such as SPD, FOR, DEX, KIN, ANA, INS, DIS, FOC, ELO, or SIG) is always better.
  • Controlling Characteristic (C) Scale: Theoretical range 1–20, playable human range 4–18, with the Human Average at C = 10.
  • Composite Controlling Characteristics (C1 / C2): Most skills combine two characteristics (such as DEX/TAC for Lockpicking, ELO/ANA for Persuasion, or DEX/SIG for Pistols):
    C = ⌊ C1 + C22 ⌋
  • Gated Supporting Characteristic (Min C3 >= X): If a skill specifies a minimum supporting characteristic and the character falls short by k = X − C3 points, each point of shortfall imposes +2 to Δext (e.g., firing Fully Automatic Weapons with FOR 8 against Min FOR >= 10 adds +4 to Δext).

3.3 Factor 3: Internal Character Modifiers (Δint ∈ [−2, +10]) — Stumble Recovery Penalty

Internal modifiers reflect the character's physiological and psychological condition (Fatigue, Wound Shock, Pain, Dehydration, Psychological Strain, or Cognitive Dissonance). Rather than penalizing the character's opening motion (Pair 1), Δint increases the negative carry-over penalty (δunder) after a missed pair:

δunder = δbase + Δint = 6 + Δint,    δover = 6

Crucially, Δint imposes zero penalty on Pair 1—if a fatigued master succeeds on Pair 1, muscle memory carries them through without slowdown; if they miss Pair 1, recovery is much steeper.

3.4 Factor 4: Known Tool Modifier (Δtool) vs. Hidden External Difficulty (Δext)

To ensure the player can roll and resolve their entire sequential 2d20 cascade step-by-step without ever knowing the true difficulty of the obstacle, Satya separates external modifiers into two components:

  1. Known Tool & Loadout Modifier (Δtool ∈ [−4, +8] — Applied to Player's TNP): Represents transparent modifiers that the character is directly aware of before starting the action—such as using a -2 masterwork toolkit, a -4 AI-assisted smart-optic, +4 improvised street tools, or an untrained/defaulting penalty (+4 to +6). This shifts the Player's Personal Target Number (TNP):
    TNP = (1.5K + 1) − CP + Δtool = 31 − CP + Δtool
    • Each +1 to Controlling Characteristic (CP) lowers TNP by 1 on every pair.
    • Each +1 to Known Tool Penalty (Δtool) raises TNP by 1 on every pair (while a -2 or -4 tool bonus lowers TNP).
  1. Hidden External Task Difficulty (Δext ∈ [−10, +16] — Applied to GM's Hidden TNGM): Represents the objective complexity of the task, hidden security countermeasures, lock tolerances, target armor/cover, environmental hazards, and weather. Instead of shifting the player's target number, Δext shifts the GM's Hidden Resistance Target (TNGM):
    TNGM = 21 − Δext    (equivalent to an opposing Controller CGM = 10 + Δext)
    • Higher Δext lowers the GM's target number TNGM, making it easier for the GM's hidden roll to score multiple resistance successes (SGM) and harder for the environment to botch (BGM).
    • Lower Δext (easy/routine conditions) raises TNGM, making GM resistance successes rare and GM environmental botches frequent!

4. Step 3 & 4: Rolling the Player's Cascade & The GM's Hidden Resistance

4.1 The Player's Sequential 2d20 Cascade (Against Known TNP = 31 − CP + Δtool)

Once NP, TNP = 31 − CP + Δtool, δover = 6, and δunder = 6 + Δint are set, the player rolls NP sequential pairs of 2d20:

  1. Pair 1 (n = 1):
    • Compute Y1 = A1 + B1 (since Carry1 = 0).
    • If Y1 > TNP (or ≥ TNP in roll-over mode):
    • Record +1 Player Success (SP += 1).
    • Set carry-over for Pair 2 to +δover (+6).
    • If Y1 ≤ TNP:
    • Record 0 Successes on Pair 1.
    • Set carry-over for Pair 2 to −δunder (−(6 + Δint)).
  2. Subsequent Pairs (n = 2 … NP):
    • Compute the modified pair sum:
    Yn = An + Bn + Carryn
    • Compare Yn against TNP:
    • If Yn > TNP (or ≥ TNP): Record +1 Player Success (SP += 1)!
    • If Yn ≤ TNP: Record 0 Successes on Pair n.
    • If Yn < 2 (dropping below the physical 2d20 minimum of snake-eyes 2 due to negative carry −δunder): Record +1 Floor Breach (BP += 1).
    • Update Carryn+1 according to the task's Carry Mode:
    • Standard Carry Mode (markov_step / CarryMode::MarkovStep — Default): Carryn+1 = +δover (+6) if Pair n succeeded, or −δunder (-4 to -16) if Pair n failed. Carry does not stack beyond a single step, allowing a skilled practitioner who stumbles on Pair 1 to fight through −δunder on Pair 2 or 3 and flip the cascade back into positive +6 momentum!
    • High-Stakes Carry Mode (cumulative_snowball / CarryMode::CumulativeSnowball): Carryn+1 = Carryn + δover on success, or Carryn − δunder on failure. Used for unforgiving high-stakes procedures (Neurosurgery, EOD Disarming, Orbital Docking) where early mistakes compound rapidly.
    • Linear Margin Carry Mode (linear_margin / CarryMode::LinearMargin): Exact difference carry (Carryn+1 = Yn − TNP).
  3. Player's Signed Margin (MP):
    • If SP ≥ 1, then MP = +SP.
    • If SP = 0, the player's closest attempt Ybest = max(Y1, …, YNP) is measured in 4-point (K/5 = 4) tiers below TNP:
    Beff = min(5,  ⌊ TNP − Ybest4 ⌋),    MP = −Beff
    (So missing by 0–3 points yields a Player Clean Miss (MP = 0), while missing by 4+ points yields a Player Botch (MP ∈ [−1, −5])).

4.2 The GM's Hidden Resistance Roll (1-Pair Tiered vs. Full Cascade)

Simultaneously, the GM resolves the opposing resistance using one of two modes (GmResistanceMode):

  1. Mode A — 1-Pair Tiered GM Resistance (GmResistanceMode::TieredSinglePair { step_size: 4 } — Default for Environment, Locks, Medical, Cyber & Hazard Tasks):
    • The GM secretly rolls a single 2d20 pair (XGM = AGM + BGM ∈ [2, 40]) against the hidden resistance target TNGM = 21 − Δext.
    • GM Resistance Successes (SGM — 4-Point Over-Tiers): If XGM > TNGM (or ≥ TNGM), the obstacle scores 1 base success plus +1 additional success for every 4 points above TNGM:
    SGM = 1 + ⌊ XGM − TNGM − 14 ⌋,    BGM = 0,    MGM = +SGM
    • GM Clean 0 or Environmental Botch (BGM — 4-Point Under-Tiers): If XGM ≤ TNGM, the obstacle scores SGM = 0 successes and 1 botch tier for every 4 points below TNGM:
    SGM = 0,    BGM = min(5,  ⌊ TNGM − XGM4 ⌋),    MGM = −BGM
    • If TNGM − 3 ≤ XGM ≤ TNGM, the GM rolled a Clean 0 (MGM = 0).
    • If XGM ≤ TNGM − 4, the GM rolled an Environmental/Obstacle Botch (MGM ∈ [−1, −5])—an unexpected flaw, brittle pin, or lucky opening!
  2. Mode B — Active Opponent Full Cascade (GmResistanceMode::FullCascade — For Opposed NPC Combat, Social & Stealth Exchanges):
    • When the task directly opposes an active NPC with their own Skill Level NNPC and Controller CNPC (e.g., Melee Parry, Counter-Surveillance, Social Interrogation), the GM rolls the NPC's full NNPC-pair cascade against TNNPC = 31 − CNPC + Δtool,NPC, producing (SGM, BGM, MGM).

5. Step 5: The 7-Way Contested Outcome Matrix (ContestedOutcomeKind & OutcomeTier)

Once both the Player (SP, BP, MP) and the GM (SGM, BGM, MGM) have rolled, compute the Net Signed Margin (Δ M = MP − MGM) and resolve the contested check using ContestedTieRule::HybridSignedMargin:

5.1 How Successes, Ties, Floor Breaches & GM Botches Interact

  • To Win Cleanly (SP > SGM): The player must roll strictly more successes than the GM's resistance roll (SP > SGM).
    • If the player suffered a Floor Breach (BP ≥ 1) during their cascade and the GM did not botch (MGM ≥ 0), the win becomes a SuccessWithFloorBreach (PartialSuccess).
    • If the GM botched (MGM < 0), the GM's botch cancels the player's floor breach and boosts the player's net margin (Δ M = SP − (−BGM) = SP + BGM), making it much easier to reach ExtraordinarySuccess (Δ M ≥ +3)!
  • Positive Ties (SP = SGM ≥ 1 ⟹ GrazingTieSuccess): When both the player and the GM score the same positive number of successes (1 vs 1, 2 vs 2, 3 vs 3), the player succeeds at a minor cost (OutcomeTier::PartialSuccess)—accomplishing the core objective, but taking +25% extra time (TC), making minor noise, or achieving reduced yield.
  • Zero Ties (SP = 0, MP = 0 vs. MGM = 0 ⟹ CleanFailure): If neither side scores a success and neither side botches (0 vs 0), the status quo holds and the player's attempt fails cleanly.
  • Lucky Break (SP = 0, MP = 0 vs. MGM < 0 ⟹ LuckyBreakSuccess): If the player rolled 0 successes without botching (a Clean Near-Miss, MP = 0) and the GM botched (MGM < 0), the obstacle's internal failure rescues the player! They scrape through with a LuckyBreakSuccess (OutcomeTier::PartialSuccess).
  • Player Botch (MP < 0 ⟹ BotchFailure): If the player botched (MP < 0), an environmental botch by the GM (MGM < 0) cannot turn a player botch into a success, though it softens the net botch severity (Δ M = MP − MGM).

6. Complementary Skills, Composite Tasks & Extended Project Clocks

6.1 Complementary Knowledge-to-Physical Synergy (KNO → PHY)

When a character uses an analytical or academic Knowledge (KNO) skill to plan, diagnose, or calculate a physical or technical execution (e.g., Clinical Diagnostics before Paramedicine, Basic Electronics before Lockpicking an electronic strike plate, Trigonometry before Precision Rifle, or Mechanical Engineering before placing Military Demolitions), resolve the KNO setup roll first:

  • 3+ Successes (ExtraordinarySuccess): Grants -4 to Δext AND boosts positive carry δover from +6 to +8 on the execution roll.
  • 1–2 Successes (Success): Grants -2 to Δext on the execution roll.
  • 0 Successes (CleanFailure): No modifier (+0).
  • Botch (CriticalFailure): Flawed assessment imposes +4 to Δext on the execution roll.

6.2 Dual-Skill Gate (Simultaneous Multi-Skill Execution)

When a single continuous action simultaneously demands two skills (e.g., FPV Kamikaze Drone Strike in Heavy Electronic Jamming requiring both Tactical Recon & Loitering Munition and Electronic Warfare / C-UAS), the character rolls a number of 2d20 pairs equal to:

6.3 Downtime, Training, Research & Extended Project Clocks

When characters are between active scenes—recovering from surgery, engineering a custom piece of hardware, decrypting an archive, or studying to raise a Skill Level (N)—Satya resolves progress using Extended Project Cascades: 1. Extended Project Progress Clock:

  • The GM sets a Project Complexity Requirement in Total Cascade Successes Needed (e.g., 10 Successes to fabricate a custom drone frame, or 15 Successes to trace a shell corporation across three offshore jurisdictions) and a Time Interval per Roll (1 Hour, 1 Day, or 1 Week).
    • At each interval, the character rolls their governing skill (2d20 cascade) and adds their signed_margin to the Project Clock:
    • Positive successes (+1 to +N) advance the project.
    • A SuccessWithComplication advances the project, but consumes extra raw materials, money, or leaves a forensic trace (+1 Heat).
    • A BotchFailure (< 0) sets progress back by the botch margin or damages a component.
  1. Post-Lifepath Skill Advancement (In-Campaign Training):
    • Improving an existing Skill from Level N to N+1 (or learning a new N = 1 skill) during a campaign uses the same Exponential Study Curve and Discipline (DIS + Mental + APT) Advancement Check defined in Lifepath Character Creation, Economy & Heat.
    • Combat, crisis, and high-stakes field experience count as accelerated study hours whenever a character succeeds (or survives a SuccessWithComplication) on a Challenging (Δext ≥ +4) task using that skill.

7. Complete Worked Examples

7.1 Worked Example 1: Sarah's Lockpicking Task (Player Cascade vs. Hidden 1-Pair Tiered GM Roll)

Scenario: Sarah needs to pick the mechanical pin-tumbler cylinder on a heavy hardened-steel bike lock in a dimly lit alley before a security patrol rounds the corner. Crucially, Sarah knows her own skill, hands, and tools, but she does not know whether the lock inside the weathered casing is a cheap standard core (Δext = 0) or fitted with anti-pick security spool pins (Δext = +4).

  1. Character Profile & Gate Check:
    • Characteristics: Dexterity (DEX) = 13 (steady hands), Tactile (TAC) = 12 (sensitive fingertips), Focus (FOC) = 11.
    • Skill: Lockpicking & Physical Security Bypass (PHY/KNO, DEX/TAC, Minimum DEX 10, TAC 10, FOC 9) at Skill Level N_P = 3 (Competent Journeyman).
    • Gate & Tools: Picking a mechanical pin-tumbler lock is a Class B (Trained / Defaultable) task with [Tool: Mandatory]. Sarah has a professional tension wrench and hook pick set (Known Tool Modifier Δtool = 0).
  2. Known Player Factors vs. Hidden GM Factors:
    • Player Skill Level (NP): 3 pairs of 2d20.
    • Player Controlling Characteristic (CP):
    CP = ⌊ DEX 13 + TAC 122 ⌋ = 12
    • Player's Known Personal Target (TNP):
    TNP = 31 − CP + Δtool = 31 − 12 + 0 = 19
    (Notice that Sarah knows her target is 19 (> 19, so 20+ on 2d20) regardless of how hard the lock is!)
    • Player Internal Modifier (Δint): Sarah is winded from sprinting two blocks (fatigue_level = 1 ⟹ Δint = +2).
    • Her positive momentum carry is δover = +6.
    • Her failure stumble carry is δunder = 6 + 2 = 8 (-8 penalty after a missed pair).
    • GM's Hidden External Difficulty (Δext): Unknown to Sarah, the lock has a hardened security spool pin (+2) and the alley is dimly lit (+2), giving a hidden Δext = +4 (Challenging).
    • The GM's hidden resistance target is:
    TNGM = 21 − Δext = 21 − 4 = 17
  3. Step A — Sarah Rolls Her N_P = 3 Sequential 2d20 Cascade (Against TNP = 19):
    • Pair 1 (n = 1, Carry1 = 0):
    • Sarah rolls A1 = 7, B1 = 11 ⟹ Y1 = 7 + 11 + 0 = 18.
    • Comparing Y1 = 18 vs. TNP = 19: Miss! Her pick slips past the first pin.
    • Because Sarah is slightly winded (Δint = +2), her stumble passes Carry2 = −δunder = −8 to Pair 2!
    • Pair 2 (n = 2, Carry2 = −8):
    • Sarah steadies her breathing and rolls A2 = 16, B2 = 14.
    • Modified total: Y2 = 16 + 14 − 8 = 22 > 19!
    • +1 Player Success (SP = 1)! Despite the -8 stumble penalty from Pair 1, her strong roll (30 raw) recovers her rhythm!
    • Under standard markov_step carry, her success flips the cascade into positive momentum: Carry3 = +δover = +6!
    • Pair 3 (n = 3, Carry3 = +6):
    • Riding the +6 momentum, Sarah rolls A3 = 9, B3 = 10.
    • Modified total: Y3 = 9 + 10 + 6 = 25 > 19!
    • +1 Player Success (SP = 2, BP = 0, MP = +2)!
    • Epistemic Moment: Sarah looks at her dice: she scored 2 Successes cleanly! She knows she executed well—but she still doesn't know if 2 successes is enough to beat the lock until the GM's hidden roll is compared!
  4. Step B — Comparing Against the GM's Hidden 1-Pair Tiered Roll (TNGM = 17, 4-Point Over-Tiers):
    • Look at how three different hidden 2d20 rolls by the GM resolve Sarah's 2 successes:
    • Case 1 — GM Rolls AGM = 10, BGM = 9 ⟹ XGM = 19 (Clean Win):
