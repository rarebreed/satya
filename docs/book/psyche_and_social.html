1. Acting Against One's Psyche: Friction Checks & Trait Drift

While players always retain agency over their characters, acting in direct violation of a deeply ingrained Psyche trait (P ≥ 13 or P ≤ 7; see Characteristics) creates internal psychological friction.

1.1 When a Psyche Friction Check Triggers

The GM calls for a Psyche Friction Check when a character attempts a deliberate action that violently contradicts one of their extreme Psyche traits:

  • A character with High Ego (EGO 16) willingly groveling, begging, or accepting public humiliation without retaliating.
  • A character with Low Selfishness (SLF 4) abandoning an innocent bystander to die for a bag of cash.
  • A character with High Conformity (CNF 16) falsifying evidence, breaking sacred protocol, or shooting a superior officer.
  • A character with Low Aggressiveness (AGG 4) executing an unarmed captive in cold blood.
  • A character with High Belongingness (BLG 16) informing on or betraying their own crew, faction, or family.
  • A character with Low Trust (TRS 4) surrendering encryption keys, weapons, or operational safehouse access purely on an unverified promise.
  • A character with High Compassion (CPS 16) torturing a prisoner, clamping or erasing a pleading sentient AI instance, or leaving wounded civilians behind for tactical convenience.

1.2 Resolving the Friction Check (DIS vs. Psyche Deviation)

See this in play: Guide 04 — Resolving a Psyche Friction Check.

  1. Compute the trait's Psyche Deviation from neutral (10):
    Δpsyche = |P − 10|
  2. The character rolls Discipline (DIS) (N_P = 1 pair of 2d20, or N_P = 2 if trained in Counter-Interrogation & Resistance (SERE)) against their personal target TNP = 31 − DIS, opposed by the GM's hidden 1-Pair Tiered Resistance Roll against TNGM = 21 − Δpsyche (4-point over-tiers):
    TNP = 31 − DIS,    TNGM = 21 − Δpsyche
  3. Contested Outcomes:
    • CleanSuccess / ExtraordinarySuccess (SP > SGM): Clean self-mastery. The character suppresses their nature and executes the action without hesitation.
    • PartialSuccess (GrazingTieSuccess, LuckyBreakSuccess, or SuccessWithFloorBreach): The character forces themselves to go through with the act, but suffers Internal Psychological Strain (+2 to Δint) for the remainder of the scene as guilt, shame, or suppressed rage gnaws at their focus.
    • CleanFailure / BotchFailure: The character balks at the threshold—freezing for 6 ticks on the Action Clock, pulling their punch, blurting telltale hesitation, or refusing to cross the line unless they spend a full turn steeling themselves at +4 to Δint.

1.3 Psyche Drift (How Behavior Reshapes Personality Over Time)

Personality in Satya is not static stone; it is shaped by lived choices and trauma:

  • Whenever a character overrides a Psyche trait on a Friction Check (or is forced by a traumatic life event or coercive interrogation to violate it), they record 1 Drift Mark alongside that Psyche trait.
  • When Drift Marks = max(2, ⌊ DIS / 4 ⌋) (typically 3 marks), the character's Psyche score permanently shifts by 1 point toward the behavior they practiced (e.g., an idealistic rookie cop with CNF 15, SLF 6 who repeatedly takes dirty money and plants evidence gradually drifts toward CNF 11, SLF 10).

2. Purpose, Principles & Conviction Surges (Pushing Past Limits)

Just as violating one's Psyche imposes friction, acting as a pure vessel of one's deepest Psyche drives or core Principles during a desperate crisis unlocks extraordinary reserves of resolve:

See this in play: Guide 04 — Conviction Surges: Pushing Past Exhaustion on Principle.

  • Triggering a Conviction Surge (Once per Scene / Session): When a battered or exhausted character (Δint ≥ +4) takes a high-stakes risk that directly embodies an extreme Psyche trait (P ≥ 15 or P ≤ 6):
    • Altruistic / Tribal Sacrifice (BLG >= 15 or SLF <= 5): Holding a doorway under heavy fire so the rest of the team can escape.
    • Boundless Compassion (CPS >= 15): Stepping into lethal danger or burning cover to shield helpless innocents, whistleblowers, or an enslaved sentient mind from cruelty.
    • Unbowed Defiance (AGG >= 15 or EGO >= 15): Refusing to stay down after taking a critical wound against a rival who mocked them.
    • Iconoclastic Truth (OPN >= 15 or CNF <= 5): Broadcasting a regime's classified war crimes while security forces breach the server room.
  • Mechanical Benefit: For that decisive action (or combat exchange): 1. Internal Fatigue and Wound Shock penalties (Δint) are reduced by -6 (allowing the character to reach the Focused/Resolved state of Δint = −2, so δunder = 4). 2. The character ignores the action_time_factor penalty from Fatigue and Wound Shock for that action and gains +2 to their Effective Tactical Pool (Peff).

3. Psychological State of Combat, Aggression & Morale

Combat is greatly affected by the psychological state of the fighters. A combatant's mind and will to fight will often be defeated before their body.

3.1 Psychological Drives (Harm:Other vs. Self:Protect)

To model the psychological state of a character under fire, Satya tracks four dynamic drives anchored by Discipline (DIS) and the nine Psyche Characteristics (AGG, SLF, EGO, BLG, CNF, OPN, EXT, TRS, CPS):

  • Fight (Harm:Other): How willing and driven you are to do harm (anchored by Aggressiveness AGG and tempered by Compassion CPS when facing helpless targets).
  • Preservation (Self:Protect): How needful you are to stay alive and unharmed (anchored by Selfishness SLF vs. Belongingness BLG and Trust TRS in allies holding the flank).
  • Rage: Acute adrenaline and emotional spike that surges Harm:Other while collapsing Self:Protect (capping Control and forcing pool points into Force + Tempo).
  • Fear: Acute threat response that spikes Self:Protect while collapsing Harm:Other (capping Force and forcing pool points into Control + Tempo, or Pure Tempo in a rout).

3.2 Motivation & Psyche Alignment (Δmot)

Fighters who have nothing to lose or are fighting for a cause greater than themselves are much harder to break than mercenaries fighting only for a paycheck. If a character's or unit's morale drops, the will to fight collapses and only defensive or self-preservation moves can be taken.

A combatant's situational Motivation Modifier (Δmot) shifts their Discipline (DIS) Morale Checks and Self-Preservation Threshold based on how the fight aligns with their Psyche:

  • Fighting for Crew, Family, or Homeland (BLG >= 14 or SLF <= 6): Grants -2 to -4 to TNeff on all Morale checks and +2 to the Tactical Pool during a Conviction Surge (e.g., holding a doorway so allies can escape).
  • Shielding Innocents / Sanctity of Life (CPS >= 14): Grants -2 to TNeff on Morale checks when directly protecting helpless beings; imposes +2 Morale/Composure friction if ordered to use indiscriminate lethal force against non-combatants. *(Conversely, Utilitarian Detachment (CPS <= 6) ignores collateral-guilt morale penalties while alienating high-CPS allies.)*
  • Mercenary / Transactional Motive (SLF >= 14 and BLG <= 8): Imposes +2 to +4 to TNeff on Morale checks as soon as the character takes a wound (wound_shock_index >= 1) or the odds turn against them—they are fighting for money, not to die.
  • Sworn Duty / Chain of Command (CNF >= 14): Grants -2 to TNeff on Morale checks while a commanding officer is present and issuing orders, but imposes +4 Morale Shock if command breaks down.

3.3 Aggressiveness (Harm:Other / Self:Protect) & Emotional Triggers

As Churchill famously said, "It's not the size of the dog in the fight, but the size of the fight in the dog." Conversely, some people are timid, meek, or conflict-averse, and either have no heart to harm others or are terrified of harm being done to themselves.

Essentially, aggressiveness is the ratio of desire to do harm to others (Harm:Other) versus willingness to be harmed (Self:Protect). Notice the distinction between desire and willingness: a berserker has a high baseline AGG, whereas a cold professional assassin (AGG 9, CPS 4) may be completely capable of killing without feeling emotional aggression unless triggered. If a character has a very low Self:Protect (SLF <= 5, BLG >= 15, or protective CPS >= 15), they are willing to sacrifice themselves to shield others or achieve their goal.

How the Balance Changes Under Fire

Aggression starts from Personality (AGG vs. SLF / CPS) and Situation (e.g., hatred of a rival faction, sense of honor when an opponent is disadvantaged, betrayal shock, or revulsion at innocent suffering), and shifts dynamically during combat. Whenever a psychological trigger occurs, the character makes a Discipline (DIS) Composure Check (2d20 cascade with TNeff = 31 − DIS + Δtrigger):

Tactical Effects of Aggression vs. Timidity

  • High Aggression / Rage (Harm:Other >> Self:Protect): Grants an adrenaline surge (+2 to Tactical Pool and ignores -2 of pain shock), but imposes tunnel vision—you must allocate at least 50% of your pool into Force, and your Control (Guard & Precision) is capped at ≤ ⌊ Peff / 4 ⌋. You become a terrifying blitz against a stunned or timid opponent, but walk straight into traps and counter-strikes against a composed master.
  • High Self-Protection / Timidity (Self:Protect >> Harm:Other): Preserves Control (Guard/Cover) and Tempo (Escape), but caps Force (Force ≤ Control), making it difficult to finish an armored or braced opponent or push through suppressive fire.

Base Morale & Rolling for Morale

A character's Base Morale Controller (Cmorale) is anchored by their Discipline (DIS, 1–20 scale) plus their situational Motivation Modifier (Δmot):

Cmorale = DIS + Δmot

Whenever a Morale Trigger occurs, the character rolls a contested 2d20 cascade check (N_P = 1 pair for civilians, N_P = 2 for hardened combat veterans or characters trained in SERE / Command) against their personal target TNP = 31 − Cmorale, opposed by the GM's hidden 1-Pair Tiered Resistance Roll against TNGM = 21 − Δmorale (4-point over-tiers):

TNP = 31 − Cmorale,    TNGM = 21 − Δmorale
  • CleanSuccess / ExtraordinarySuccess / PartialSuccess (SP ≥ SGM or LuckyBreakSuccess): The character holds their nerve and remains at their current Morale Condition (or rallies +1 step on ExtraordinarySuccess with net margin Δ M ≥ +2).
  • CleanFailure (SP < SGM with MP ≥ 0): Morale drops by 1 Condition Step (e.g., Steady → Wavering, or Wavering → Broken).
  • BotchFailure (MP < 0): Morale drops by 2 Condition Steps (e.g., Steady → Broken, or Wavering → Panic).

4. Social Combat, Debate & Cognitive Dissonance ("Mental Wounds")

Satya treats high-stakes debates, ideological arguments, courtroom cross-examinations, boardroom takeovers, and interrogations with the same tactical depth as physical firefights. Just as physical combat targets a body's structural integrity and blood volume, Social Combat targets a character's Psyche to inflict Doubt, Ego Wounds, and Cognitive Dissonance.

4.1 Reading the Target (Empathy & Read Person Setup)

Before launching a decisive argument, a skilled negotiator or manipulator sizes up their counterpart:

  • Rolling Empathy & Read Person (INS/ITU) or Investigative Elicitation (INS/ELO) opposed by the target's Deception (ELO/CRE) or Discipline (DIS) reveals the target's most salient Psyche poles:
    • 1 Success: Reveals the target's single most extreme Psyche trait (e.g., Belongingness 16).
    • 2 Successes: Reveals two extreme Psyche traits (e.g., Belongingness 16 and Openness 16) and whether they are in potential tension.
    • 3+ Successes (ExtraordinarySuccess): Reveals the target's full Psyche profile and grants -2 to Δext on the subsequent social maneuver.

4.4 Worked Example of Cognitive Dissonance: High Openness vs. High Belongingness

Consider an NPC scientist or mid-level syndicate lieutenant, Marcus:

  • Marcus's Relevant Psyche Profile:
    • Openness (OPN) = 16 (Deeply curious, respects hard evidence and rational merit; Resonance -6 to logical proof).
    • Belongingness (BLG) = 16 (Deeply attached to his faction/peer group; Resistance +6 against any belief that would alienate him from his peers).
    • Discipline (DIS) = 11, Focus (FOC) = 11 (Composure Threshold = 5).
  • The Debate Exchange: 1. The player character, Nadia, wants to convince Marcus that his faction's leadership falsified clinical trial data and is knowingly killing patients—a belief that Marcus's entire peer group fiercely denies. 2. Nadia presents undeniable forensic logs and biochemical proof using Persuasion & Rhetoric (ELO/ANA). 3. The Dual-Psyche Wedge:
    • Because the evidence has undeniable intellectual merit, it resonates with Marcus's Openness 16 (Δext, res = −6).
    • Because accepting this truth means turning against his tribe and facing total ostracism, it clashes with Marcus's Belongingness 16 (Δext, clash = +6).
    • The two modifiers cancel to Δext = 0 on the initial roll—but the wedge is armed!
    4. Nadia rolls her cascade and scores 2 net successes over Marcus's counter-argument. 5. The Result — Severe Cognitive Dissonance:
    • Marcus takes 1 Doubt fracture AND 2 stacks of Cognitive Dissonance (dissonance_index = 2).
    • Inside Marcus's mind, his Openness 16 knows Nadia's data is real, while his Belongingness 16 is terrified of being cast out by his friends and colleagues.
    • At the start of the next exchange, the Cognitive Dissonance bleeds for +4 Δint (δunder jumps from 6 to 10), making it nearly impossible for Marcus to mount a coherent defense if he misses Pair 1.
    • How Marcus Resolves the Dissonance: To stop the psychological bleeding, Marcus is forced into one of four Dissonance Resolution Paths: 1. Conversion / Defection (Openness Wins): If Nadia follows up with Charm & Rapport (PRE/ELO) or offers him a new community of truth-seekers (soothing his Belongingness), Marcus converts—handing over the master encryption keys and drifting his BLG toward Nadia's group. 2. Dogmatic Denial (Belongingness Wins): If Nadia instead insults his colleagues (triggering a defensive tribal reaction), Marcus slams the door in Dogmatic Denial, clearing the dissonance by permanently burning a point of his own Openness (OPN 16 -> 15) via Drift to protect his tribal identity! 3. Compartmentalization (Secret Informant): Marcus outwardly conforms to his group while secretly leaking data to Nadia (+4 Δint whenever forced to act against his conscience). 4. Emotional Lashing Out: If pushed while high in AGG or EGO, Marcus snaps to silence the source of his inner torment.
