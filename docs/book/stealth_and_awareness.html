Stealth, Passive Awareness, Active Observation & Investigation

Rust Engine Implementation: crates/satya-engine/src/action_catalog_stealth.rs (ObservationMode, ActiveSensoryChannel, ObserverAttentionState, AlertnessLevel, AcousticSurfaceKind, AmbientNoiseMask, ThermalStealthProfile, DigitalRfHygiene, SocialStealthWardrobeFit, TailStance, ForensicDecayContext, ClueDiscoveryTier, compute_watch_duty_fatigue_delta_ext, evaluate_alertness_transition, evaluate_multi_spectrum_stealth_modifiers, evaluate_surveillance_tail_exchange, evaluate_forensic_clue_search). Companion Chapters: Action Economy & OODA Loop (action_economy_and_ooda.md) · Characteristics & Senses (characteristics_and_aptitudes.md) · Lifepaths, Cover Identities & Safehouses (lifepaths_and_economy.md).

In Satya, stealth, detection, surveillance, and crime-scene investigation do not collapse into a single "Perception vs. Sneak" binary roll. An operative sneaking across wet gravel in pitch darkness faces different acoustic, thermal, and RF constraints than a clean operative walking past security checkpoints under a forged biometric legend—and a bored security guard scrolling on a tablet processes sensory evidence completely differently from a hunter actively sweeping a room with thermal optics.

1. Passive Awareness vs. Active Observation (ObservationMode)

Satya enforces a strict mechanical boundary between Passive Awareness (background notice while your attention is elsewhere) and Active Observation (deliberately committing OODA Ticks to sweep a sector, audit a person, or search a room).

TIP

GM Pacing Rule on Partial Stealth Results:

  • CleanSuccess (ΔM = +1..+2): Holds AlertnessLevel unchanged.
  • GrazingTieSuccess / LuckyBreakSuccess / SuccessWithFloorBreach: Advances AlertnessLevel by +1 step without blowing cover—turning Level 0 into tense Level 1 cat-and-mouse instead of an instant combat slog!
  • CleanFailure: Advances +2 steps (Level 0 → Level 2, Level 1+ → Level 3).
  • BotchFailure: Jumps straight to Level 3: Located / Compromised.

3. Multi-Spectrum & Social Stealth

In 2035 operational environments, hiding from unaided human eyes is only the first layer. Satya tracks Visual/Illumination, Acoustic Surface & Ambient Noise, Thermal/FLIR Signature, Digital/RF Emissions, and Social Camouflage ("Hiding in Plain Sight") uniformly through evaluate_multi_spectrum_stealth_modifiers.

  • Gait & Encumbrance (KIN / ITU):

3.3 Thermal / IR Signatures & Digital RF Hygiene

  • Thermal Signature (ThermalStealthProfile):
    • UnshieldedStandard: +0 vs. FLIR (+0 visual penalty once ThermalFlir cuts darkness/smoke).
    • HotEquipmentOrSuppressor: -4 Δext bonus to a FLIR observer (freshly fired suppressors, hot motors, and idling exhaust glow white-hot against nocturnal backgrounds).
    • ThermalBaffledCloak: +6 Δext penalty to a FLIR observer and restores full illumination/smoke concealment against thermal optics.
  • Digital & RF Signature Hygiene (DigitalRfHygiene):
    • AirGappedFaradayPouch: +0 electronic footprint; completely silent to IMSI catchers and BLE beacons.
    • AirplaneModePassiveWearable: +2 Δext vs. RF/sensor sweeps (unshielded crystal/NFC leakage).
    • ActiveSmartphoneOrBleTrail: +4 Δext AND automatically pings 2035 municipal/corporate checkpoint logs whenever the bearer carries Operational Heat >= 3 (lifepaths_and_economy.md §3.2).

4. Surveillance, Tail Craft, Forensic Clues & Post-Truth Verification

4.1 Urban Surveillance & Surveillance Detection Routes (SDR)

Trailing a target through a city or running counter-surveillance (SDR) to spot a tail resolves across 15–30 minute route legs using Surveillance, Tail Craft & Counter-Surveillance (FOC / SIG) (Class B — untrained operatives default from Observation at +4 Δtool):

Tail Stance (TailStance)Detection Risk Shift (SDR)Tracking Difficulty ShiftTactical Trade-Off
LooseTail (2+ blocks back, swapping jackets, parallel lanes)-2 (Harder for target to spot)+4 (Higher risk of losing target at metro transfers)Prioritizes operational secrecy over continuous visual lock.
TightTail (Within 15–30m, crossing same lights/doors)+4 (High risk of burning tail)-2 (Easier to hold contact)Required in dense crowds/rain; any GrazingTie / FloorBreach burns the shadower on an SDR.
  • ExtraordinarySuccess (ΔM >= +3): Completes the route leg in 15 min cleanly and grants -2 Δext on the following leg.
  • CleanFailure: Target slips the tail at a blind corner or transit transfer without noticing (+4 to re-acquire).
  • BotchFailure: Target both slips the tail and photographs/logs the shadower (target_burned_shadower = true).

4.3 2035 Post-Truth Verification: Deepfakes & Synth-Media Audits

In 2035, audio recordings, bodycam feeds, and biometric logs are routinely fabricated by AI 2.0 generative pipelines. Auditing disputed media requires Digital Forensics & DFIR (ANA / FOC) or AI 2.0 Synth-Media (ANA / INS), modified by forensic tool quality (-2 specialized workstation, +4 smartphone viewer only) against the forger's Net Margin (Δext set by forger skill). Scoring CleanSuccess identifies manipulation artifacts; scoring ExtraordinarySuccess (ΔM >= +3) extracts the generator watermark, model family, or origin hardware signature!

At the Table: Night Infiltration vs. Bored Sentry (Action + Multi-Spectrum + Alertness)

Mara (KIN 13, ITU 12 -> C = 12, Stealth N = 3, Tier-1 Encumbrance +0) creeps (StealthCreep) across wet courtyard gravel (+4) during steady rain (-2 ambient mask) in moonlight (-6 visual concealment) inside a Faraday pouch (+0) wearing a thermal-baffled cloak (+6 vs FLIR). Her net movement modifier is +4 - 2 = +2 Δext (TNP = 31 − 12 = 19 for Stealth, while the guard faces hidden difficulty shifts). Meanwhile, the guard has been standing post for 5 hours (Watch Fatigue +2) on RoutinePatrolBored (+2 attention, N = 1 passive), with derived AWR = 11. If Mara ties on a GrazingTieSuccess, the guard does not magically open fire—he escalates to Level 1: Anomaly / Suspicious (+4 Surprise Freeze, shifts to HighAlertHunting with full N and -2 attention) and walks over with a flashlight to check the gravel!
