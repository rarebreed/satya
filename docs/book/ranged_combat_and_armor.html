1. Why Guns Don't Feel the Same in Satya: Granular Engineering Architecture

In most tabletop RPGs, firearms are reduced to interchangeable reskins of the same stat block (1d8 or 2d6 damage, a flat range increment, and a magazine size). A cheap street-printed 9mm pistol, a match-grade competition handgun, a loose-tolerance stamped 7.62×39mm AKM, and a free-floated direct-impingement 5.56mm carbine feel virtually identical at the table.

Satya rejects generic weapon dice. Every firearm is a physical machine governed by four coupled engineering causal chains: 1. Internal & External Ballistics ( → Kinetic Energy Ek): How projectile mass (mproj), gunpowder charge (mprop in grains), barrel length (Lbbl), muzzle devices (suppressors, muzzle brakes), and the action's gas seal (etaaction) generate muzzle velocity and retain kinetic energy downrange. 2. Terminal Ballistics ( → Trauma Magnitude, Penetration & Wound Channels): How impact kinetic energy (Ek), projectile caliber (dcal), bullet construction, and inverse accuracy / projectile wobble (Wyaw) interact with armor plates (Pen vs. PR & BABT) and soft tissue (permanent crush cavity, hydrostatic shock, and erratic tumbling/keyholing wound channels). 3. Mechanical & Ergonomic Engineering ( → Accuracy Acc, Recoil R, & Handling TC): How action type, weapon weight (Mwpn), Center of Gravity relative to the chamber (ΔCoG), Overall Length (OAL), barrel length (Lbbl), barrel quality (Qbbl), barrel mounting (e.g., free-floating vs. clamped), mechanical tolerances (τtol), and ammunition quality (Qammo) govern marksmanship precision and recoil recovery. 4. Tribology, Tolerances & Feed Mechanics ( → Reliability Rel, Clear Jam Time TCclear, & Strip Time TCstrip): How action geometry, feed mechanism (box magazine, drum, belt, tubular, cylinder, single-shot), machining tolerance (τtol — which helps accuracy but hurts reliability!), and manufacturing quality (Qmfg) determine malfunction thresholds and how many Count-Up Ticks it takes to clear a jam or field-strip the weapon.

2. Ranged Combat in the Tactical Triad (Tempo, Control, Force)

Ranged combat uses the exact same Tempo, Control, and Force triad on the Count-Up Action Clock (1 Tick = 0.25s, 4 Ticks = 1.0s) as close-quarters combat:

  • Tempo (T — Aim Speed, Target Acquisition & CQB Handling): Governs how many ticks (TC) you spend bringing the weapon to bear—from a fast point-shoot or hip-fire snapshot (high Tempo, lower TC) to a deliberate multi-tick sighted shot (low Tempo, higher Control). Modified by the weapon's Overall Length (OAL) and Center of Gravity (ΔCoG).
  • Control (C — Sight Alignment, Trigger Squeeze & Cover Exposure): Governs marksmanship precision (DEX/SIG or DEX/FOC), called shots to bypass armor, AND how much of your body is exposed out of cover while firing (Guard).
  • Force (F — Stance, Recoil Bracing & Volume of Fire): Governs how rooted and braced your shooting platform is (FOR/MAS) to absorb the weapon's physical Recoil Rating (R) during rapid or automatic fire (Fully Automatic Weapons DEX/FOR) and project suppressive pressure.

3. Ammunition Architecture & Internal/External Ballistics

Every cartridge in Satya is defined by six physical and operational parameters that drive both kinetic energy and weapon handling:

  1. Caliber (dcal in mm / inches): The bore diameter and frontal cross-section of the projectile (e.g., 9×19mm, .45 ACP [11.43mm], 5.56×45mm, 6.5mm Creedmoor [6.72mm], 7.62×39mm, 7.62×51mm NATO, 12-Gauge [18.5mm]).
  2. Projectile Mass (mproj in grains gr): Mass of the bullet (1 gram = 15.432 grains; 1 grain = 0.0648 grams). Combined with caliber, this defines Sectional Density (SD = mproj7000 · din2)—how much mass sits behind each square millimeter of frontal area to punch through armor and resist air drag.
  3. Gunpowder / Propellant Charge (mprop in grains gr): The mass of chemical propellant inside the cartridge case. Propellant grains provide the thermochemical energy that accelerates the bullet down the barrel—and also create the high-pressure gas jet that adds to recoil and muzzle blast!
  4. Bullet Profile & Construction:
    • Full Metal Jacket (FMJ / Ball): Standard lead/copper-alloy core in a gilding-metal jacket (εexp = 1.0, standard penetration).
    • Jacketed Hollow Point (JHP / Expanding): Mushrooms on soft-tissue impact to 1.5×–1.8× caliber diameter (+1 Caliber Crush Ical, -2 Armor Penetration Pen).
    • Open-Tip Match Boat-Tail (OTM / Match): Aerodynamic boat-tail with precision jacket concentricity (+1.0 Accuracy, extended energy retention Ddecay).
    • Armor-Piercing (AP — Tungsten-Carbide / Hardened Steel Penetrator): Sub-caliber dense core (+2 to +3 Armor Penetration Pen, -1 Soft-Tissue Crush Ical on clean through-and-through hits unless it yaws).
    • Subsonic Heavy Load: Reduced powder charge (mprop) paired with a heavy projectile so muzzle velocity stays below Mach 1 (< 340 m/s), eliminating the supersonic ballistic crack when paired with a suppressor (-25% to -40% Ek).
    • Overpressure (+P / +P+ / Hybrid-Case High-Pressure): +15–25% higher powder charge/chamber pressure (+1 IKE or Pen, +1 Recoil R, -1 Reliability Rel from accelerated extractor/parts wear).
  5. Ammunition Quality (Qammo):
    • Salvaged / Corroded / Street Hand-Loads (Q_ammo = -1.5): Inconsistent powder weights and seated depths (-1.5 Accuracy, -1 Reliability, +1 Wobble Wyaw).
    • Bulk Steel-Cased / Military Surplus (Q_ammo = -0.5): Bimetal jacket, lacquered steel case (-0.5 Accuracy, -1 Reliability in tight chambers due to lower case obturation/extraction lubricity).
    • Standard Commercial Brass / Mil-Spec Duty (Q_ammo = 0.0): Baseline (0.0 Accuracy, 0 Reliability).
    • Match-Grade / Precision Hand-Loaded (Q_ammo = +1.0): Extruded temperature-stable propellant weighed to ± 0.02 gr and concentric cases (+1.0 Accuracy).
  6. Feed System & Capacity: How ammunition is stored and presented to the breech (detailed in Section 3.3).

3.1 How Kinetic Energy (Ek) Is Calculated

The Kinetic Energy at Impact (Ek, measured in Joules J) is not a static lookup—it emerges from the cartridge's bullet mass (mproj), gunpowder charge (mprop), barrel length & muzzle devices (etabbl), action gas seal (etaaction), and target distance (d):

Ek(d) = Echem(mproj, mprop)Cartridge Base Energy × etabbl(Lbbl, Muzzle)Barrel & Muzzle Factor × etaactionAction Gas Factor × e−d / DdecayAerodynamic Retention

1. Cartridge Base Thermochemical Energy (Echem)

Each grain of smokeless propellant releases ≈ 260 J of chemical energy. The fraction transferred into the projectile depends on the mass ratio between the bullet (mproj in gr) and the expanding powder gas (mprop in gr):

Echem ≈ 105 · mprop · (mprojmproj + 2.2 mprop)   [in Joules]
  • Why Both Bullet Mass and Powder Grains Matter:
    • .45 ACP has a massive 230 gr bullet but only 5.6 gr of powder: the heavy slug extracts a high fraction of that small charge (Echem ≈ 560 J) at slow, subsonic velocity (260 m/s).
    • 5.56×45mm NATO has a light 62 gr bullet backed by 27.0 gr of powder: the huge powder-to-bullet ratio drives the projectile to ~920 m/s (Echem ≈ 1,850 J).
    • 6.5mm Creedmoor pairs a long, sleek 140 gr bullet with 42.5 gr of powder (Echem ≈ 3,300 J at 835 m/s), while 7.62×51mm NATO (.308) pairs a 147–168 gr bullet with 45.0 gr of powder (Echem ≈ 3,500 J).

2. Barrel Length (etabbl) & Muzzle Device Effects

Gunpowder requires barrel length (Lbbl) to burn completely and expand behind the bullet. Every cartridge class has an Optimal Burn Reference Barrel Length (Lopt):

  • Pistol / SMG Calibers (9mm, .45 ACP): Lopt = 12.0 cm (4.7 in)
  • Magnum Handgun / PDW (5.7×28mm, .357 Mag): Lopt = 16.5 cm (6.5 in)
  • Intermediate Rifles & Shotguns (5.56×45mm, 7.62×39mm, 12-Gauge): Lopt = 45.7 cm (18.0 in)
  • Full-Power Battle & Precision Rifles (6.5mm Creedmoor, 7.62×51mm): Lopt = 56.0 cm (22.0 in)
  • Magnum & Anti-Materiel Rifles (.338 Lapua, .50 BMG): Lopt = 68.5 cm (27.0 in)
etabbl = clamp(0.60,  1.15,  1.0 + 0.35 ln(LbblLopt)) + Δetamuzzle
  • Short-Barrel Velocity Loss & Blast: Cutting a 5.56×45mm barrel down from 50.8 cm (20") to a 26.2 cm (10.3") CQB Mk18 drops etabbl from 1.04 down to 0.81 (-22% kinetic energy!) because unburned powder detonates outside the muzzle—imposing +2 Muzzle Flash / Indoor Concussion (Tinnitus risk)!
  • Muzzle Attachments (Δetamuzzle & Handling):
    • Bare Crown / Thread Protector: Δetamuzzle = 0.00, Recoil +0, Length +0 cm.
    • Flash Hider / Birdcage: Δetamuzzle = 0.00, Recoil -0.5, suppresses night-vision flash bloom, Length +4 cm.
    • Muzzle Brake / Compensator: Redirects propellant gas laterally/upward against baffle plates: Δetamuzzle = −0.01, Recoil R reduced by -1 to -2, Length +5 cm, but vents a violent overpressure wave sideways (+2 Tinnitus/Dazzled hazard to anyone within 2m of the muzzle indoors!).
    • Sound Suppressor / Silencer: Traps and cools expanding gases inside internal baffles: Δetamuzzle = +0.02 ("Freebore Boost"), Recoil R reduced by -1, reduces gunshot hearing detection (HEA) by -8 (-12 with subsonic ammo) and eliminates indoor Tinnitus, but adds +16 to +20 cm to Overall Length (OAL), adds +0.40 to +0.55 kg at the muzzle tip (shifting ΔCoG forward by +1 tier), and increases gas blowback/carbon fouling in Direct Impingement actions (-1 Rel after 60 rounds unless using an adjustable gas block or flow-through suppressor)!

4. Terminal Ballistics: How Kinetic Energy, Caliber & Inverse Accuracy Determine Damage

3. Inverse of Accuracy: Projectile Wobble & Erratic Wound Channels (Wyaw)

In real-world terminal ballistics, a bullet that flies with laser-tight gyroscopic stability (High Accuracy, Acc ≥ +2) strikes nose-first at a 0circ angle of attack. If it is a non-expanding FMJ or AP bullet, it often drills a clean, narrow "icepick" channel straight through soft tissue before dumping its kinetic energy.

Conversely, a bullet fired from an inaccurate weapon (Acc < 0—due to loose combat tolerances, worn/short rifling, non-free-floated barrel whip, or poor-quality surplus ammo) exits the crown with significant nutation and precession (wobble/yaw). Although this wobble makes the round harder to place on small targets at long range, any wobbling round that hits a target strikes at an oblique angle of attack—causing the bullet to immediately tumble end-over-end (keyholing), deform, or tear a wide, jagged, erratic wound channel through muscle, organs, and blood vessels!

Wobble / Yaw Factor (Wyaw) = clamp(0,  3,  max(0,  1 − Acc) + Δ Wspitzer)

(where Δ Wspitzer = +1 for high-aspect-ratio spitzer bullets like 5.56×45mm or 7.62×39mm M67 impacting at > 700 m/s).

  • Mechanical Effect of Wyaw: 1. Erratic Soft-Tissue Cavitation: Adds +Wyaw directly to Raw Ballistic Impact against unarmored targets (or when armor is penetrated), and if Wyaw ≥ 2, increases the resulting Hemorrhage Grade by +1 (up to Grade 4 Arterial)! 2. Sideways Armor Strike Penalty: Because a wobbling bullet strikes hard armor slightly sideways rather than point-first, it suffers −⌊ Wyaw / 2 ⌋ to Armor Penetration (Pen)!

4.2 Unified Ballistic Impact, Penetration & Wound Tier Formulas

Combining all three terms yields the weapon's Raw Ballistic Impact and Base Armor Penetration (Pen):

Raw Ballistic Impact = IKE + Ical + Wyaw

5. Firearm Architecture, Accuracy (Acc) & Recoil Engineering

A weapon's Intrinsic Accuracy (Acc ∈ [−4, +4]) measures its mechanical precision (dispersion in Minutes of Angle, MOA, where 1.0 MOA ≈ 2.9 cm at 100m). In Satya, Acc is not an arbitrary bonus—it is the sum of seven physical engineering factors:

Acc = round(ΔAccaction + ΔAccweight + ΔAccCoG + ΔAccbbl_len + Qbbl + ΔAccmount + τtol + Qammo)
  • How Acc Works in the 2d20 Cascade:
    • On any Deliberate Sighted / Aimed Shot (or Braced Overwatch shot), the weapon's Acc applies directly to the shooter's Known Tool Modifier (Δtool = −Acc, so a +3 Accuracy rifle lowers TNP = 31 − CP + Δtool by -3, while a -2 Accuracy street gun raises TNP by +2!).
    • On a rushed Single Snap-Shot / Point-Shoot, positive barrel/scope accuracy above +1 cannot be exploited (capped at +1), though negative accuracy (Acc < 0) still penalizes the shot in full—and always feeds into the projectile's Inverse Accuracy Wobble (Wyaw = max(0, 1 − Acc))!

5.2 Weight, Center of Gravity (ΔCoG), Length (OAL), Barrel Specs & Tolerances

1. Weapon Weight (Mwpn) & Recoil Physics (R)

By conservation of momentum, the free recoil velocity of a firearm is inversely proportional to its mass (Mwpn in kg), while the upward torque depends on the cartridge impulse (mproj, mprop):

Base Weapon Recoil (Rwpn) = max(1,  round(0.025 · mproj + 0.14 · mprop√Mwpn) + Δ RCoG + Δ Rmuzzle + Δ Raction)
  • How Weapon Weight Affects Accuracy (ΔAccweight):
    • Ultralight (< 0.65 kg Pistol or < 2.4 kg Rifle): Easy to carry all day, but violent recoil impulse causes sight jump and shooter flinch (-0.5 Accuracy, high Rwpn).
    • Standard Duty Weight (0.7–1.2 kg Pistol; 2.8–4.0 kg Carbine/Rifle): +0.0 Accuracy.
    • Heavy Target / Bull-Barrel Chassis (> 1.3 kg Pistol; 4.5–7.5+ kg Precision Rifle / LMG): Mass damps micro-tremors and recoil impulse (+0.5 to +1.0 Accuracy when braced, reduces Rwpn by -1 to -2), at the cost of Encumbrance (kg) and +1 Exertion Mark per 20 Ticks if fired standing unsupported!

    • Close-Quarters Crowding (Reach 1 Clinch & Tight Interior Corridors):
    • In Clinch Range (Reach 1) or confined spaces (vehicles, ventilation shafts, narrow stairwells), any weapon with OAL > 50 cm suffers +1 Δext and +1 TC per 20 cm over 50 cm (because the long barrel must be canted or pulled back across the chest to clear doorways, and gives a close opponent a long lever to grab/deflect with +2 to their Disarm Control!).

Manufacturing Quality (Qmfg)

While tight tolerance reduces reliability, superior metallurgy, surface treatment, and quality control (Qmfg) can preserve reliability—at a steep financial cost:

  • Q_mfg = -2 (Improvised / 3D-Printed Street Polymer / Pipe Scrap): Soft unhardened steel or FDM polymer frames that warp under heat; -2 Rel, 0.2× base cost.
  • Q_mfg = -1 (Budget Clone / Worn Third-World Surplus): Cast zinc/pot-metal small parts, pitted chambers, weakened extractor springs; -1 Rel, 0.5× base cost.
  • Q_mfg = 0 (Standard Commercial / Mil-Spec Issue): Forged 7075-T6 aluminum / 4140 ordnance steel, Parkerized or nitride finish; +0 Rel, 1.0× base cost.
  • Q_mfg = +1 (Duty-Grade Tier-1): Cold-hammer forged, magnetic-particle inspected (MPI) bolt, chrome/phosphate coated carrier, enhanced extractor springs; +1 Rel, 1.8× base cost.
  • Q_mfg = +2 (Masterwork / Aerospace DLC & NP3 Coated): Precision wire-EDM machining coated in self-lubricating Diamond-Like Carbon (DLC) or Nickel-PTFE (NP3) with dry-film micro-lubricity; +2 Rel (allowing a gunsmith to build a +1.0 Tight-Tolerance Match weapon that still has net positive reliability!), 3.5×–5.0× base cost.

6.2 When Malfunctions Trigger, Clear Jam Time (TCclear) & Strip Time (TCstrip)

Satya integrates weapon malfunctions directly into the 2d20 Cascade Outcome Tiers without requiring a separate roll on every shot:

  1. Minor Malfunction (Stovepipe / Failure-to-Feed / Misfire):
    • Trigger: On SuccessWithComplication (total_successes >= 1 AND raw_botches >= 1), if raw_botches > Rel, the current shot resolves normally, but the weapon fails to cycle the next round!
    • Clearance (Immediate Action — TCclear, minor):
    • Double-Action Revolver: 2 Ticks (0.5s) (pull trigger again to rotate past the dead primer!).
    • Pump-Action / Bolt-Action: 4–6 Ticks (1.0–1.5s) (rack slide/bolt to eject spent shell).
    • Box-Fed Pistol / Standard Carbine (DI/Piston/Striker): 8 Ticks (2.0s) ("Tap, Rack, Bang" — slap magazine baseplate, rack charging handle/slide to eject stovepipe).
    • Bullpup Rifle: 10 Ticks (2.5s) (must break cheek weld to inspect/clear rear ejection port).
    • Drum-Fed / Belt-Fed: 12 Ticks (3.0s).
  2. Major Malfunction (Double-Feed / Bolt-Override / Stuck Casing / Link Jam):
    • Trigger: On BotchFailure (total_successes == 0 AND effective_botches >= 2), if effective_botches + Fouling > 2 + Rel, the shot fails to fire and two rounds wedge against the feed ramp (or a spent case rips its rim off inside the chamber).
    • Clearance (Remedial Action — TCclear, major): Requires a DEX/ANA (Gunsmithing or weapon skill) check while spending TCclear, major ticks (20–24 Ticks [5–6s] for box-fed pistol/carbine; 28–32 Ticks [7–8s] for bullpup/drum; 36 Ticks [9s] for tubular shell-lifter jam or belt-fed link jam) to lock the bolt back, rip out the magazine/belt, cycle the action clear, and re-insert a fresh feed.
  3. Field-Strip Time (TCstrip):
    • Represents the time required to disassemble the weapon into its primary field-strip groups (receiver/frame, barrel, slide/bolt-carrier, recoil assembly, feed unit) for Preventative Maintenance (30 min cleaning grants +1 Rel for the next combat scene), clearing a catastrophic squib/mud obstruction in the field, or breaking a rifle down to fit inside a covert courier bag:
    • Quick-Takedown Lever / Captive Pin (Glock, SIG P320, AKM, Steyr AUG): 28–40 Ticks (7–10 seconds).
    • Push-Pin Hinged Receiver (AR-15/M4, HK416, SCAR, MP5): 48–72 Ticks (12–18 seconds).
    • Barrel Bushing / Interlocking Link (1911 Government, Remington 870, Revolvers): 120–160 Ticks (30–40 seconds).
    • Precision Chassis / Multi-Fastener / Belt-Fed GPMG (M240B, Mk14 EBR, Accuracy International): 240–480 Ticks (60–120 seconds).

8. Granular Armor Engineering, Penetration (PR) vs. BABT & Degradation

In traditional RPGs, armor either makes a character harder to hit ("Armor Class") or subtracts a flat number from a generic Hit Point pool. In reality, armor adds physical Mass (kg), Thermal Insulation (Heat Retention), and Encumbrance, while acting as a material barrier over specific anatomical hit locations that stops penetration up to its structural limit (PR)—yet still transfers kinetic momentum (Behind-Armor Blunt Trauma, BABT) into the wearer's ribs, sternum, or skull!

8.1 Armor Engineering Properties & Material Physics

Every piece of protective gear is defined by six granular attributes: 1. Anatomical Coverage (1d20 Hit Locations): Which exact sub-zones from the Master Hit Location Table the armor physically covers. Uncovered zones (or gaps targeted via Control Called Shots) receive 0 Protection! 2. Material Architecture & Multi-Hit Degradation:

  • Soft Ballistic Weave (Para-Aramid / Kevlar / Twaron / Dyneema): Catch-net of high-tensile fibers. Stops wide, low-sectional-density pistol bullets (PR_Ballistic = 4, PR_Edged = 2), but narrow high-velocity rifle spitzers (5.56mm, 5.7×28mm, 6.5mm Creedmoor) or icepick thrusts (PR_Icepick = 1) part the fibers and punch straight through! Flexible (BTD = 2, transfers high blunt deformation into the ribs).
    • Rigid UHMWPE (Ultra-High-Molecular-Weight Polyethylene): Compressed unidirectional polyethylene laminate. Very lightweight (floats on water!), absorbs multi-hit 5.56mm / 7.62×39mm FMJ (PR_Ballistic = 6, BTD = 4), but defeated by tungsten/hardened-steel Armor-Piercing (AP) cores.
    • Monolithic / Mosaic Ceramic Strike Face (Boron Carbide / Silicon Carbide + UHMWPE Backer — NIJ Level IV / RF3): Ultra-hard ceramic shatters and blunts incoming high-velocity rifle/AP projectiles (PR_Ballistic = 8, BTD = 5), but fractures locally on impact (-1 to -2 PR degradation per rifle hit on that plate!).
    • 2035 Non-Newtonian Shear-Thickening Fluid (STF) + Nanotube Myomer Carapace: Flexible under normal movement, instantaneously locking into a rigid lattice upon supersonic impact (PR_Ballistic = 9, BTD = 8, powered exoskeleton offsets its own weight).
  1. Penetration Resistance (PR_Edged & PR_Ballistic): Structural threshold required to perforate the armor.
  2. Blunt Trauma Damping (BTD): How much kinetic energy the rigid trauma plate and closed-cell foam pad dissipate before the remainder crushes the wearer's underlying bones and organs (BABT).
  3. Mass (kg), Ergonomic Load Multiplier & Concealability:
    • Well-fitted body armor distributes its mass across the torso/hips, counting at ×0.8 of its physical weight toward the wearer's Encumbrance Tier (or subtracted by a powered exoskeleton's load offset).
  4. Don / Doff Time & Cost ($ & [Access]): How quickly the armor can be put on or emergency-jettisoned (e.g., pulling a plate carrier's quick-release cable in deep water takes 8 Ticks [2.0s]).

8.2 Resolving Armor vs. Incoming Hits (Penetration vs. BABT)

When an attack hits an armored Hit Location with Net Successes Δ M ≥ 1: 1. Compute Effective Penetration and Effective Raw Impact:

Effective Pen = Base Pen + ⌊ Δ M2 ⌋,    Effective Raw Impact = Raw Impact + ⌊ Δ M2 ⌋
  1. Compare Effective Pen against the Struck Zone's Active PR:
    • Case A — Armor Stops Penetration (Effective Pen ≤ PR): The blade or bullet does not perforate the skin (Zero Bleed, causes_bleed = false). However, the kinetic momentum deforms the backface of the armor, inflicting Behind-Armor Blunt Trauma (BABT):
    Blunt Trauma (BABT) = max(0,  Effective Raw Impact − BTD)
    • Example: Taking a 7.62×39mm AKM hit (Raw Impact = 9, Base Pen = 5) on the chest of a Level IV Ceramic Plate Carrier (PR = 8, BTD = 5) with Δ M = 2 (Effective Pen = 6 <= 8, Effective Raw Impact = 10):
    • The ceramic plate stops the bullet (Zero Bleed — saving the wearer from a fatal pierced heart/lung!).
    • However, the wearer suffers BABT = 10 − 5 = 5 (Tier 2 Blunt Chest Trauma: cracked rib, -5 to Force Cap, +1 wound_shock_index, and the ceramic plate cracks by -1 PR down to PR 7).
    • Case B — Armor Overmatched (Effective Pen > PR): The projectile punches through the armor with reduced energy:
    Penetrating Trauma = max(0,  Effective Raw Impact − PR)
    • This inflicts a penetrating Structural Wound & Hemorrhage (Grades 1–4) and degrades the struck armor zone's PR by -1 (or -2 if Effective Pen − PR ≥ 4).

9.2 Worked Example 2: Engineering Comparison — Loose-Tolerance 7.62×39mm AKM vs. Free-Floated Bolt-Action 6.5mm Creedmoor at 400 Meters

Why does a sniper choose a heavy, tight-tolerance bolt-action 6.5mm Creedmoor (#11 in Catalog) over a rugged 7.62×39mm AKM (#9 in Catalog) for a 400-meter overwatch shot against an armored target wearing a UHMWPE Patrol Carrier (PR_Ballistic = 6, BTD = 4)?

  1. Aerodynamic Energy Retention at d = 400 m & Action Gas Efficiency:
    • AKM (7.62×39mm, 123 gr bullet, 25.5 gr powder, 41.5 cm clamped barrel, Long-Stroke Piston etaaction = 0.97, Ddecay = 420 m):
    • Muzzle Energy E0 = 2,040 J, but at d = 400 m, air drag reduces impact energy to:
    Ek(400 m) = 2,040 × e−400 / 420 = 787 J ⟹ IKE = 3 (dropped from 5 to 3!)
    • Because its loose combat tolerance (τtol = −1.0) and non-free-floated barrel give it Acc = −1 (3.8 MOA), its mechanical dispersion cone at 400m is 43 cm wide (+4 Δext range dispersion + +1 Δtool from Acc -1), and its wobbling bullet (W_yaw = 2) has its Armor Penetration reduced at 400m to Base Pen = 3—which cannot penetrate the target's PR 6 Patrol Plate!
    • AT-X Precision Rifle (6.5mm Creedmoor, 140 gr Match OTM, 42.5 gr powder, 61.0 cm Free-Floating Match Barrel, Bolt-Action etaaction = 1.00, Ddecay = 950 m):
    • Sealed bolt loses zero gas (1.00×) and the 61 cm barrel extracts +3% bonus velocity (E0 = 3,260 J). Thanks to the sleek boat-tail bullet's 950m decay scale, impact energy at 400m is still:
    Ek(400 m) = 3,260 × e−400 / 950 = 2,140 J ⟹ IKE = 5
    • Its free-floated match barrel (+1.0 Mount, +1.5 Barrel), bolt action (+1.5), heavy chassis (+0.5), tight tolerance (+1.0), and match ammo (+1.0) yield Acc = +4 (0.35 MOA — a 4 cm group at 400m!), lowering the sniper's TNP by -4, while its high sectional density (+3) gives Base Pen = 7 even at 400 meters—punching clean through the target's PR 6 plate!
  2. When the Polarity Flips (Muddy Trench CQB at 15 Meters):
    • In a flooded, mud-choked trench at 15m (Environmental Fouling = +2), the sniper rifle's 112 cm Overall Length imposes +3 CQB crowding penalty, its bolt action adds +4 Ticks (1.0s) between every single shot, and its tight match chamber requires careful maintenance.
    • Meanwhile, the AKM's Rel = +4 (+2 Long-Stroke Piston, +1 Loose Tolerance, +1 Rugged Mfg) completely ignores 1–4 raw botches and +2 mud fouling without jamming, cycles semi-auto or full-auto with zero bolt-cycling delay, and its W_yaw = 2 wobbling 7.62mm rounds inflict horrific Raw Impact = 9 (Tier 4) erratic wound channels at close range!
