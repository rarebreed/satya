Satya TTRPG Rules Book — Summary & Table of Contents

This manifest defines the canonical chapter ordering, identifiers, categories, cross-references, and Rust engine module mappings for the Satya Computer-Assisted RPG (CARPG) Rules Book. It is consumed by:

  • The Satya Rust Engine & Web Frontend (satya-engine, satya-signaling /api/rules, and the satya-web Left Tabbed Notebook Rules Book pane)
  • The Interactive Marimo Rules Book & Static HTML Generator (notebooks/satya_rules_book.py & python -m satya_ml.build_rules_book)
  • The Satya AI Game Master (query_satya_rules in src/satya_ml/ai_gm/tools.py)

Table of Contents

Part I: Foundation & Mathematical Calibration (01-foundation/)

  1. An RPG for a More Civilized Age: Design Philosophy (id: design_philosophy)
    • Origins of Satya, realism vs. escapism, and how Satya solves 17 structural flaws of traditional RPG systems.
  2. Mathematical Notation, Variables & Formula Glossary (id: notation_glossary)
    • Canonical reference for every mathematical variable, subscript (_P, _GM, _n, _eff), dice/cascade symbol (TNP, TNGM, δover, δunder, Δ M), attribute abbreviation, OODA/Action Clock metric (Dcog, TC), and Tactical Triad formula (Pbase, Peff, Tmax, Cmax, Fmax) used across the rules book.
  3. Probability & Calibration of the Cascading Dice Mechanic (id: probability)
    • Mathematical foundation, distribution topology (skewness, modality, tail behavior), why K = 20 outperforms K = 10 and K = 100, C vs. N sensitivity, avoiding the "Skilled Botch Paradox," and Always-Contested Task Resolution via the 1-Pair Tiered GM Resistance Rule (4-pt over/under tiers).

  1. Cascading 2d20 Engine & Task Resolution (id: tasks)
    • Canonical Two-List Cascading (2d20) dice mechanic, Player Personal Target TNP = 31 − CP + Δtool vs. Hidden GM Resistance Target TNGM = 21 − Δext (1-Pair Tiered GM Resistance), carry modes (markov_step, cumulative_snowball), 7-Way Contested Outcome Matrix (CleanSuccess, GrazingTieSuccess, LuckyBreakSuccess, SuccessWithFloorBreach, BotchFailure), 5-step Task Pipeline, Tool Tiers, Gate Classifications (Class A / B / C), Extended Project Clocks, and Sarah's contested lockpicking walkthrough.
  2. Master Task Requirements Catalog (id: task_requirements_catalog)
    • Canonical Section 8 reference table defining gate classes, primary/complementary skills (Nmin), controlling characteristics (CP), mandatory/situational tools (Δtool), and hidden GM resistance modifiers (Δext) across all skill categories.
  3. Action Economy, OODA Loop, Time Scales & Stealth (id: action_economy)
    • Temporal calibration (1 Tick = 0.25s, 4 Ticks = 1s, 20 Ticks = 5s Tactical Window), Full OODA Loop & Cognitive Decision Score (Dcog — "Quick Thinking"), Count-Up Tick Clock mechanics, Base Time Cost (Base TC) reference tables, action_time_factor, OODA action pivots, and Multi-Sensory Stealth & Detection (SIG, HEA, OLF, ITU).

  1. Characteristics, Senses, Psyche & Aptitudes (id: characteristics)

  2. Skill System & Hierarchical Skill Trees 1–4 (id: skills_and_trees)
    • Talents (TAL) vs. Physical (PHY), Knowledge (KNO), and Hybrid (PHY/KNO) skills, Multi-Category taxonomy, [Access] restriction tiers, Prerequisite Rules, and Hierarchical Skill Trees 1–4 (Mathematics, Physical Sciences/Trades, Computing/AI 2.0, and Biology/Medicine/Biotech).
  3. Hierarchical Skill Trees 5–8: Combat, Vehicles, Social & Tradecraft (id: skills_trees_tactical_and_social)
    • Hierarchical Skill Trees 5–8: Unarmed/Melee/Firearms/Heavy Ordnance (Tree 5), Athletics/Vehicles/Aviation/Drones (Tree 6), Social/Influence/Interrogation/PsyOps (Tree 7), and Literacy/Law/Intelligence/Tradecraft/Artistic (Tree 8).
  4. Lifepath Character Creation, Socioeconomic Upkeep & Heat (id: lifepaths_and_economy)

Part V: Combat Architecture, Close Quarters & Firefights (05-combat/)

  1. Combat Architecture, Tactical Triad & Pools (id: tactical_triad)
    • Combat design philosophy, anatomical trauma over Hit Points, Count-Up Action Clock overview, and the "Envelope & Fill" Tactical Pool Architecture (Pbase = Ccombat + 3N) across Tempo, Control, and Force (Tmax, Cmax, Fmax).
  2. Melee Combat, Close-Quarters Tactics & The Tactical Triad (id: melee_combat)
    • Close-quarters anatomy, Tempo / Control / Force in melee, Guard & Seizing the Initiative, Root vs. throws/sweeps, the Six Core Tactical Allocations (TC, TF, CF, Pure T/C/F), Melee Weapon Reach & Profiles, and the Viktor vs. Elena & Clinch/Throw walkthroughs.
  3. Granular Ballistics, Ammunition Physics & Firearm Engineering (id: firearms_and_ballistics)
    • Four coupled firearm engineering chains, Ammunition & Internal/External Ballistics (mproj, mprop powder grains → impact kinetic energy Ek, ηbbl, ηaction), Master Ammunition Reference Table (9mm to .50 BMG), Feed Systems, Terminal Ballistics (IKE + Ical + Wyaw inverse-accuracy tumbling wound channels), Action Types, Weight, Center of Gravity (ΔCoG), Overall Length (OAL), and Free-Floating vs. Clamped Barrel Specs.
  4. Ranged Combat, Weapon Reliability, Master Catalogs & Armor (id: ranged_combat_and_armor)
    • Ranged Fire Modes & Recoil Bracing (F ≥ Reff) in the Tactical Triad, the Accuracy–Reliability Tolerance Trade-Off (τtol vs. Rel), Malfunctions (Stovepipe vs. Double-Feed), Clear Jam & Field-Strip times, Master Granular Firearms Catalog (12 benchmark weapons), Body Armor Engineering (PR vs. BABT), and worked firefights (Mateo vs. Sgt. Vance & AKM vs. 6.5mm Creedmoor at 400m).

Part VI: Health, Trauma, Movement, Vehicles & Survival (06-health-and-survival/)

  1. Health, Anatomical Trauma, Fatigue & Mental Conditions (id: health_and_trauma)

  2. Movement, Encumbrance, Vehicles & Environmental Survival (id: movement_and_environment)
