Electronic Warfare, 2035 Surveillance, ECM & ECCM

Rust Engine Implementation: crates/satya-engine/src/electronic_warfare.rs (SurveillanceNodeKind, SURVEILLANCE_NODE_CATALOG, NetworkIntrusionPhase, evaluate_network_or_sensor_intrusion, EcmMethodId, ECM_METHOD_CATALOG, EccmHardeningId, ECCM_HARDENING_CATALOG, evaluate_ecm_vs_eccm_contest, EwHardwareRole, evaluate_custom_ew_hardware_design), reusing crates/satya-engine/src/engineering_design.rs and crates/satya-engine/src/robotics_and_ai_design.rs (SyntheticMetabolicMode, bridged in crates/satya-campaign/src/awakened_ai_substrate.rs). In Satya, fighting 2035 surveillance and autonomous hunter-killer networks is governed by the physics of the electromagnetic spectrum: active RF jamming is never invisible concealment. Flooding a city block with broadband noise jams unshielded drones, yet turns the jammer itself into a blinding beacon for passive Direction-Finding (DF) receivers (+R_DF). Operatives must weigh passive optical/adversarial spoofing and physical Faraday air-gapping against the risk of tripling Heat and summoning a kinetic response.

1.2 Four-Phase Intrusion Workflow & The Hard Air-Gap Rule

Technical infiltration (Network Exploitation or Signals Intelligence under ANA / CRE) steps cleanly through NetworkIntrusionPhase: 1. ReconAndFootprint (+0 Δext) → 2. CredentialOrExploitEntry (+2) → 3. LateralMovementAndEscalation (+4) → 4. PayloadOrFeedInjection (+4).

  • Hard Air-Gap Rule: Any AirGappedScadaEnclave blocks remote exploitation (access_blocked_by_air_gap = true) unless is_physically_tapped = true (requiring an operative or manipulator-arm drone to plug in a hardware implant, optical bridge, or acoustic/EM side-channel probe).
  • IDS Trace (IDStrace) & Heat Fallout:
    • ExtraordinarySuccess: gains -2 Δext setup advantage into the next intrusion phase with 0 trace.
    • PartialSuccess / GrazingTie / CleanFailure: adds +1 IDStrace.
    • BotchFailure: adds +3 IDStrace and triggers immediate +trip_heat Operational Heat on the station alert board.

  • Contested Difficulty Formula: hidden_ext = (P_ECCM - P_ECM) * 2 + range_penalty, where operating beyond effective_radius_m adds +4 Δext per extra radius bracket (max +12).
  • Optical / Fiber Immunity: OpticalLaserOrFiberTether (burst laser or spooling fiber-optic FPV micro-drone) is immune to active RF jammers, requiring physical line-of-sight blocking, scissors, or a FaradayMeshEnclosureTarp over an enclosed bay.
  • Splitting Embodied AI Uplinks: Successfully landing an uplink-severing ECM attack forces remote-tethered AI 2.0 proxies from QuantumTetheredSprint down to SyntheticMetabolicMode::SeveredFaradayReflex (robotics_drones_and_ai_architecture.md §3).
  • Direction-Finding Exposure (RDF): Every active RF emitter accumulates:
RDF = sigbeacon + ⌊ tactive,min2.0 ⌋ + bonusDF

When RDF ≥ 10 (or RDF ≥ 6 on any Partial/Failed jam), passive spectrum hunter arrays lock the emitter's firing line within seconds.

5. Worked Example: Tight-Beam Jamming, Fiber Immunity & Custom Jammer Pod

AT THE TABLE

1. Severing an FHSS Corporate Hunter-AI Uplink at 90 m for 3.0 min:

  • Matchup: DirectionalTightBeamJammer (PECM=5, 150 m radius, Rbase=3) vs. FhssFrequencyHoppingSpread (PECCM=3, +1 DF bonus).
  • Result: Within range (90 m <= 150 m, 0 range penalty), Net Advantage +2 (hidden_ext = -4). On a Clean Win: target_uplink_severed = true, dropping the drone/AI substrate into SyntheticMetabolicMode::SeveredFaradayReflex. Triangulation index RDF = 3 + ⌊ 3/2 ⌋ + 1 = 5—below lock thresholds (triangulation_lock_triggered = false).

2. Contrast — Broadband Barrage vs. AiAdaptiveCognitiveRadio2035 (110 m, 4 min) & Fiber FPV:

  • Broadband (80 m radius) at 110 m suffers +4 range attenuation and drives RDF = 8 + ⌊ 4/2 ⌋ + 3 = 13 ≥ 10, immediately locking the operator's position (triangulation_lock_triggered = true).
  • Fiber / Optical Drone (OpticalLaserOrFiberTether): Active RF jamming is blocked (blocked_by_optical_or_fiber_immunity = true).

3. Designing a Baffled PortableJammerPod (CommercialCncMachineShop, CustomSubassembly, Masterwork):
