3.1 Human–AI Co-Design & Verification Debt (Dverify)

When an engineer delegates heavy design load (ai_autonomy_leverage LAI ∈ [0, 6]) to an AI co-pilot during an engineering Project Clock:

  • Velocity Boost: Every winning sprint gains +floor(L_AI / 2) bonus Project Clock successes.
  • Overreach Gap Penalty: Whenever AI leverage exceeds the engineer's genuine domain skill (Gover = max(0, LAI − Nhuman)), the unseen complexity raises the GM's hidden resistance (+G_over + hallucination_risk_delta_ext) and accrues Verification Debt (Dverify):
    • CleanSuccess / ExtraordinarySuccess: adds Gover Debt (-1 if NeuroSymbolicVerifier, minimum 0).
    • PartialSuccess / GrazingTie / LuckyBreak / FloorBreach: adds G_over + 2 Debt (-1 for NeuroSymbolicVerifier) and flags latent_bug_or_drift_risk when G_over >= 2.
    • BotchFailure: 0 Clock progress, +G_over + 4 Debt, and guaranteed latent bug/alignment drift.
  • Scrubbing Debt (evaluate_verification_debt_audit): Engineers must pause shipping to run formal test harnesses (Software Engineering or Neural Architecture check against D_verify + hallucination_risk). CleanSuccess scrubs 3 Debt (5 on ExtraordinarySuccess); NeuroSymbolicVerifier grants -2 Δtool.

4. Cyber-Prosthetics & Myomer/Neural Augmentations

In keeping with Satya's hard medical realism, cybernetics do not use an abstract "Humanity" or "Essence" currency. Instead, clinical myomer prosthetics, subdermal ballistic meshes, and cortical OODA co-processors provide concrete biomechanical gains balanced by three grounded engineering/clinical constraints: surgical/immunosuppressant rejection (Δint), Proprioceptive Friction at 4+ replaced systems (Δext on high-nuance social/psyche alignment), EMP vulnerability, and ongoing clinical maintenance cost.

4.2 Rejection Physiology, Proprioceptive Friction & EMP Hardening

  1. Immunosuppressant Rejection (Rreject → Δint):
    • Compliant patients taking anti-rejection regimen suffer 0 Δint up to 2 major implants, then +1 Δint per extra system (count - 2, plus max(0, -Q_surg) if installed in a substandard ripperdoc clinic), capped at +6 Δint.
    • Missing Immunosuppressant Doses: Raises rejection straight to count + max(0, -Q_surg) Δint (exposed directly via ConditionSource).
  2. Proprioceptive Friction (>= 4 Implants): Replacing 4 or more anatomical systems imposes (count - 3) * 2 Δext on subtle empathy/social-attunement and Psyche-alignment checks due to synthetic motor cadence and micro-expression lag.
  3. EMP Hardening (+25% Install Cost): Unshielded neural/myomer implants lock up for 20 Ticks (5.0 s) on a heavy EMP hit; clinical Faraday-shielded wiring reduces reboot latency to 4 Ticks (1.0 s).

1. Shrouded Tactical Recon Quadrotor:

  • Base: TacticalQuadrotorFpv (1.80 kg, 1.50 kg max payload, 95 Wh, 140 W, 28.0 m/s, $3,200) + ElectroOpticalFlirGimbal (0.45 kg, 18 W) + AcousticNoiseShroud (0.30 kg, 12 W, +4 HEA) + MeshRelayTransceiver (0.20 kg, 10 W) + 20 W onboard SLAM autonomy.

2. Human–AI Co-Design Sprint (L_AI = 4 vs. N_human = 2):

  • Using a SparseMoETransformer (-3 Δtool, +2 hallucination Δext), the Overreach Gap is 4 − 2 = 2. Even if the sprint succeeds cleanly (+3 Clock Progress including +floor(4/2) acceleration), +2 Verification Debt accrues until audited by a NeuroSymbolicVerifier sweep.

3. Clinical Augmentation Loadout (3 Systems, EMP-Hardened, Compliant, Qsurg = +1):

  • MyomerCyberArm + CorticalOodaCoProcessor + CochlearCommsAndAcousticFilter yields +2 Force Cap, +2 D_cog, +2 OODA, +2 HEA, flashbang tinnitus immunity, install cost $130,000 (+25% EMP shielding), upkeep $1,040/mo, +1 Δint rejection (or +3 if doses missed), 0 proprioceptive friction, and 4-Tick EMP reboot.
