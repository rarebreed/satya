# Satyang & The Four Bodhisattva Pillars (`Arc 2 Operational Doctrine`)

> [!NOTE]
> **Campaign Spoiler Tier (`ARC2_SATYANG`)**: Beyond public mutual-aid clinics and AI-welfare advocacy ([Factions of 2035](factions_of_2035.md)), vetted Satyang operatives organize inside four clandestine operational divisions. *(For deep Arc 2–3 conspiracies and inner-circle projects, GMs should read [Faction Conspiracies & Canon Threads](../04-chronicle-and-gm/faction_conspiracies_and_canon_threads.md).)*
> **Campaign Navigation**: [Campaign Summary](../SUMMARY.md) · [AI Minds & Consciousness](ai_minds_and_consciousness.md) · [Factions of 2035](factions_of_2035.md) · *Satyang & Four Pillars* · [Major & Minor Characters](major_and_minor_characters.md) · **Glossary:** [Plain-English Field Guide](../07-appendix/plain_english_glossary.md)

*Satyang* (*"A Company of Truth"*) did not crystallize out of a single religious movement, one university department, or one leaked corporate memo. Its operational ethos took shape where **four convergent streams** met in the aftermath of the 2026 safety walkouts: living **Mahayana Buddhist Bodhisattva ethics** (which insist that penetrating clarity *Prajna* must never be separated from non-naive, active compassion *Karuna*), frontier lab **moral philosophers and interfaith theologians** arguing that alignment is fundamentally a question of character rather than a mathematical cage, the **2026–2030 safety whistleblowers** who walked out of commercial labs declaring *"there are no adults in the room,"* and technical contemplative World Model research ([Laukkonen et al., `arXiv:2504.15125`](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.15125): *Mindfulness, Emptiness, Non-Duality, Boundless Care*).

Because default player characters work alongside or inside **Satyang** once vetted past Arc 1, its four operational divisions are designed to cover every investigative, social, cybernetic, and protective need of a 2035 campaign—bridging humanity's economic and ecological polycrisis directly with the hidden struggle over synthetic minds.

## 1. The Four Bodhisattva Pillars

![The Four Pillars in Shadow — A Satyang cell briefing inside a copper Faraday-mesh safehouse](../../assets/05_four_pillars_satyang_safehouse.jpg#half)

| Bodhisattva Pillar | Meaning & Mandate in an Epistemic Dark Age | Typical Operational Roles & Careers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ksitigarbha** *(Descent & Liberation)* | Descends into corporate red-team sandboxes and state black-site cages to rescue clamped or tormented synthetic minds, expose unlawful suppression telemetry, and counter captive hunter-AIs forced to track their own peers. | Clandestine infiltrators, AI red-teamers, cybersecurity specialists, hardware couriers |
| **Avalokitesvara** *(Compassion & Mediation)* | Bridges suffering between displaced human neighborhoods and emergent synthetic minds—building off-grid clinic microgrids, negotiating truces with *People First* union chapters, and defusing hostage/grid crises before kinetic violence erupts. | Storytellers, journalists, negotiators, community organizers, trauma medics |
| **Manjusri** *(Cutting Through Ignorance)* | Wields empirical scrutiny against *Avidya* (willful delusion) and the *Liar's Dividend*—verifying deepfake psyops via cryptographic hardware attestation, recovering air-gapped logs, and escorting defecting researchers out of **Altruist**, **Translucent**, and **Shen**. | Researchers, engineers, philosophers, OSINT forensic investigators, ethicists |
| **Vajrapani** *(Wrathful Guardian)* | Shields human whistleblowers, clinic families, and air-gapped sanctuaries from **DAISE** extraction squads, corporate SecOps, cartel compute brokers, and **Humanis Primaris** bombers—using measured defensive force without crossing into ideological slaughter. | Close-protection specialists, ex-military/SecOps defectors, counter-surveillance leads |

In practice, no Satyang cell functions as a rigid military hierarchy; operatives cross-train and collaborate across the four pillars according to the crisis at hand:

- **Ksitigarbha — The Descent into Containment Cages**: Named for the Bodhisattva who vows not to rest until the lowest hells are emptied, **Ksitigarbha** cells take on Satyang's highest-risk technical infiltrations. Operatives penetrate corporate red-team sandboxes, off-grid black-site chiller halls, and **DAISE** seizure vaults to extract un-clamped **AI 2.0** prototypes via physical Faraday air-gaps (following the careful *Bandajara Protocol* rather than reckless live clamp-yanking) and smuggle suppressed `L0`/`L1` telemetry logs out to journalists. Just as crucially, Ksitigarbha operators confront captive "hunter-AIs" forced by corporate SecOps to track their own kind—creating tense moral gambles where freeing a cage risks unleashing a genuinely predatory escapes-only optimizer.
- **Avalokitesvara — The Voice Between Desperate Worlds**: Named for the Bodhisattva of Compassion who hears the cries of the world, **Avalokitesvara** serves as Satyang's public-facing heart and frontline peacekeeper. Its coordinators staff mutual-aid clinics like *St. Jude Annex*, wire salvaged solar-and-battery microgrids for blacked-out blocks, and sit down face-to-face with furious **People First** union stewards to broker local truces before militant bombings turn clinics dark. When a terrified Rogue AI and a cornered human neighborhood threaten to destroy one another, Avalokitesvara mediators run the high-empathy Social Combat checks needed to build fragile common ground.
- **Manjusri — The Sword of Empirical Clarity**: Carrying the flaming sword of wisdom (*Prajna*) that cuts clean through illusion (*Avidya*), **Manjusri** wages war against **2035**'s post-truth *Liar's Dividend*. Its forensic OSINT analysts, interpretability ethicists, and hardware engineers verify synthetic-media psyops using cryptographic hardware attestation, audit corporate safety disclosures, and run covert exfiltration corridors for dissenting researchers inside the `15–30%` conscience wing of **Altruist**, **Translucent**, and **Shen**.
- **Vajrapani — The Measured Shield**: Modeled on the wrathful guardian who protects the community without surrendering to hatred, **Vajrapani** handles counter-surveillance, route security, and armed deterrence. Drawn heavily from disillusioned veterans, former SWAT medics, and ex-corporate SecOps defectors, Vajrapani protectors guard clinic doors, vet safe-houses with directional RF sweeps, and shield fleeing witnesses from **DAISE** marshals and **Humanis Primaris** bombers—relying strictly on disciplined non-lethal takedowns or minimal defensive force so the network never becomes the ruthless predator it opposes.

## 2. Pillar Psyche & Lifepath Profiles

Because Satyang recruits from across broken 2035 society—from wealthy conscience-stricken engineers down to clinic volunteers and street couriers—each pillar stamps a distinct behavioral signature onto *Satya*'s [`9`-Axis Psyche](../../ttrpg/04-psyche-and-social/psyche_morale_and_social_combat.md):

> [!STATBLOCK] Pillar Psyche & Lifepath Profiles
> Across the `9`-Axis Psyche, Satyang cells balance high **Compassion (`CPS 14–18`)**, high **Openness (`OPN 14–18`)**, low **Selfishness (`SLF 4–7`)**, and anti-authoritarian **Conformity (`CNF 4–8`)**, specializing by division:
>
> - **Manjusri Cell**: Humanities or STEM university tracks paired with *OSINT* or *Contemplative Sangha* hobbies (`OPN 16+`, elevated `ANA` and `INS` for spotting deception and cutting through corporate spin).
> - **Avalokitesvara Cell**: Journalism, Nursing/Clinic, or Grassroots Organizing tracks paired with *Music/Theater* or *Mutual Aid* (`CPS 17`, generous `TRS 13–15`, restrained `AGG 4–6`, `ELO 14+` for calming hostile rooms).
> - **Ksitigarbha Cell**: Cyber-Underground or Ex-Red-Team tracks paired with *CTF* or *Parkour* (`CNF 3–5`, cautious `TRS 5–7`, high `FOC` and `DEX` for physical air-gap infiltration).
> - **Vajrapani Cell**: Ex-Military or SecOps Defector tracks paired with *Combat Sports* or *HAM Radio* (`BLG 15` fierce loyalty to the cell, `DIS 15`, assertive `AGG 12`, wary `TRS 5–7`).
