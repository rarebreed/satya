# Major characters

## Satyang

### Dr. Budi Bandajara

Budi's formative years were in Malaysia where he grew up just outside of Johor Baru.  His father was of Irish and Canadian ancestry and worked in Singapore
in economics at an international firm.  His mother was Chinese and Malay, and did acturial work for a company in Johor Baru.  His mother grew up steeped in both
Buddhist and Muslim traditions while his father was for the most part secular, though baptized as a Catholic.  Due to his parents work, he had a keen mind for
numbers and he initially thought he would go into business like his parents.  But a college math teacher changed his mind on that.

One day while in an introductory computer science class, he was introduced to the famous Halting Problem.  Before learning of this, Budi had always assumed
that software and computers had no limit other than processing power and memory.  He asked his professor more about where this idea came from, and his professor
told Budi to also look up Kurt Godel and Turing's professor Alonzo Church.  Thus began a love not for math from a business perspective, for math itself and
how computers worked.  He was inspired by Edward Dijkstra's quote that "Computer Science has as much to do with computers as Astronomy does with telescopes".
This expanded Budi's to abstract computation, and wonder what the mind was itself.

Growing up, Budi had mostly been secular like his father, even though his Muslim grandfather had often pushed him to go to the Mosque.  But now, it was his
Chinese grandmother who he turned to, asking her more about Buddhism and many of its concepts about mind.  His grandmother advised him to learn from his uncle
who was a teacher in Penjak Silat and also a Buddhist layperson.

Budi trained his body and mind with his uncle and always enjoyed spending time at his Gelanggang.  When Budi went off to college in Singapore, he would always
make time to see his uncle and train with his old friends again.

#### Education

Budi went to a Chinese Education system in Johor Baru and speaks fluent Mandarin, Bahasa, and English as well as a little Japanese.  For college, Budi went
to the National University of Singapore for his Undergrad degree in Mathematics.  He then did a small 4 year stint in the Royal Malaysian Army in the Signal
Squadron.

After leaving the military, he went back to the University to pursue advanced degrees in Robotics and Mathematics (PhD)

#### Description

Dr. Bandajara is in his early 40s, is Eurasion, having a (mostly) European father with trace amounts of native indigenous Canadian ancestry, and a Chinese and 
Malay mother.  He is fairly tall at 180cm, with a slightly mesomorphic build.  He still trains in Silat to keep in shape.  His hair is heavily white and grey
at the sides, showing some premature aging to his hair.

Dr. Bandajara tends to look a bit more asiatic than European, with more of hooded eyes.  He does however have a more square jawline.

#### Personality

Budi is definitely an introvert, unlike his father.  Budi always had his head in the clouds and would often dream about how things could be.
