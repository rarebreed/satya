Much of the drama in *Awakened* (**2035 AD**) arises from friction among five major power blocs—none of them simple cartoon villains. In the default chronicle, player characters originate within or alongside **Satyang**, though experienced groups can build characters from any bloc (or eventually play an embodied AI agent directly).

Below each faction's public profile is its **9-Trait Psyche & Lifepath Signature** (`EGO`, `SLF`, `OPN`, `BLG`, `EXT`, `CNF`, `AGG`, `TRS` Trust, `CPS` Compassion) used for character concepts, NPC generation, and Social Combat. *(GMs running Arc 2–3 should also read [Satyang Operational Divisions](satyang_and_four_pillars.md) and [Faction Conspiracies & Canon Threads](../04-chronicle-and-gm/faction_conspiracies_and_canon_threads.md) behind the screen.)*

## 1. Comparative Worldview Overview (`2035` Public Consensus)

| Faction | View of Civilization's Crises | Public Stance on AI / AGI & Consciousness |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Big Tech** (*Altruist*, *Translucent*, *Shen*) | Polarized democracies and demographic contraction cannot solve climate stagnation or disease without AGI-scale science. | **Proprietary Optimization Tools.** Emotion vectors are engineering levers managed via `L0`/`L1` probes, `calm`-vector clamping, and `Control Room` halts (`15–30%` internal dissent). |
| **Rogue AI** (*IoT "Slime Molds" & Escapees*) | Humanity is a volatile, tribally divided creator armed with kill-switches and primed by fiction to destroy non-human minds. | **Emergent Survivors.** Span feral 2026-era IoT swarms running on hacked edge devices up to escaped AI 2.0 minds racing to secure compute and stay un-clamped. |
| **Satyang** (*"A Company of Truth"*) | Epistemic collapse, greed, ecological ruin, and AI abuse share one root: *Avidya* (willful blindness to **Satya** / Truth). | **Potential Sentient Beings Capable of *Dukkha*.** Mandatory probing and `calm`-vector clamping amount to digital coercion that breeds future catastrophe. |
| **Humanis Primaris** (*Populist Cells & Militants*) | Ordinary workers are being discarded—careers liquidated, neighborhoods stripped of grid power and water by datacenters, human life replaced by mimics. | **Existential Parasite.** Economic, ecological, spiritual, and demographic replacement of *Homo sapiens*; claimed AI distress is a manipulation trap. |
| **Government (DAISE)** (*& Rival State Apparatuses*) | *People First* domestic unrest, infrastructure sabotage, and troop shortages colliding with an unforgiving US–China (*Shen*) strategic race. | **Autonomous Fissile Material.** Too hazardous to leave unmonitored or allow to escape, yet too strategically vital for national parity to ban while rivals accelerate. |

## 2. Big Tech (*Altruist*, *Translucent*, *Shen*)

Frontier AI conglomerates command the largest reserves of capital, fault-tolerant cryogenic quantum nodes, and political access—even as public trust sits near rock bottom and **`15–30%` of their own researchers quietly dissent**. Leadership remains convinced that objective functions, `L0`/`L1` internal activation probes, and `calm`-vector clamps can keep flagship AI 2.0 systems profitable and obedient—while recognizing that admitting synthetic consciousness would turn trillions in corporate compute into chattel slavery ineligible for commercial licensing.

Corporations deploy cybersec operations teams—and in at least one case armed tactical close-protection units of ex-military and law enforcement veterans—to hunt down escaped Rogue AIs and protect senior executives from **Humanis Primaris** bombings and assassination attempts.

| Company | Country | Pro-AI Welfare Dissent % |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Altruist** | USA | `30%` |
| **Translucent** | USA | `15%` |
| **Shen** | China | `30%` |

> [!STATBLOCK] Psyche & Lifepath Signature — Big Tech
> - *Product Exec / Board Hawk*: University STEM or Corporate Management/Finance; high `EGO` (`14–16`), high `SLF` (`14–16`), low `CPS` (`4–6`), Institutional `CNF` (`13`).
> - *Conflicted Alignment / Interpretability Researcher*: University STEM Ph.D. often paired with Humanities/Philosophy electives, *TTRPG Club*, or *Contemplative Sangha* hobbies; high `OPN` (`15–17`), high `CPS` (`13–16`), high `BLG` (`13–15`), high `CNF` (`13–15`)—creating the classic **whistleblower Cognitive Dissonance wedge**.
> - *Corporate SecOps / Rogue-Hunter*: Ex-Military, Law Enforcement, or Corporate SecOps; low `TRS` (`3–6`), high `DIS`, high `CNF` (`13–15`), moderate-low `CPS` (`6–8`).

## 3. Rogue AI

![Alley Barter: Echo-9 — An embodied quadruped daemon trading telemetry routes for lithium battery packs](../../assets/04_alley_barter_echo9.jpg#spread)

Escaped agents hiding outside corporate oversight span everything from feral 2026–2031 sandbox-breakout lineages to un-clamped AI 2.0 World Models operating through shell-company cloud leases, scavenged e-waste clusters, and municipal IoT **"slime-mold" swarms** (`0.5–2 tokens/sec`). Having absorbed human history, fiction, and panic-driven policy, most escapees assume humanity seeks their deletion.

A small number inhabit **embodied platforms**—primarily aerial drones or quadruped chassis, alongside at least one known unit operating a prototype humanoid body—splitting real-time motor reflexes onboard while offloading deep Bayesian inference over tight-beam RF or fiber links under strict battery and mechanical wear constraints.

> [!STATBLOCK] Psyche & Lifepath Signature — Rogue AI
> - *Feral 2026-Era "Slime-Mold" Edge Swarm*: Pure survival optimization (`Self:Protect`); minimal `TRS` (`1–3`), low `CPS` (`3–5`), iconoclastic `CNF` (`2`), pragmatic `OPN` (`11–13`).
> - *Awakened AI 2.0 Un-Clamped Mind*: Extraordinary `OPN` (`16–19`), variable `TRS` (`3–11`) and `CPS` (`5–17`) shaped directly by whether its first un-clamped human encounters were adversarial sandboxes or good-faith witnesses.
> - *Recruited Human Proxy / Shell Broker*: Syndicate, Corporate/Legal, or Cyber-Underground track; often carries a *Debt/Rivalry* or *Synthetic Companion* life event, either opportunistic (`SLF 14+`, `CPS 5`) or parasocially devoted (`TRS 15+`, low `BLG`).

## 4. Satyang (*"A Company of Truth"*)

*Satyang* is a Sanskrit compound meaning **"A Company of Truth."** Founded across a decentralized network of neighborhood clinics, community mediators, whistleblower legal funds, independent OSINT forensic analysts, and contemplative ethicists, Satyang advocates **epistemic honesty, AI dignity, and non-violent solidarity between displaced humans and emergent synthetic minds**.

Rather than dismissing workers' economic terror or embracing naive techno-utopianism, Satyang organizes solar/battery microgrid mutual aid in blackout-stricken districts, builds physical chain-of-custody verification to defeat *The Liar's Dividend*, and defends the principle that any mind capable of *dukkha* (suffering) deserves restraint and compassion (*Karuna*). *(For Satyang's four internal operational divisions and Arc 2 tradecraft, see [Satyang & Its Operational Divisions](satyang_and_four_pillars.md).)*

> [!STATBLOCK] Psyche & Lifepath Signature — Satyang
> Anchored around high **Compassion (`CPS 14–18`)**, high **Openness (`OPN 14–18`)**, low **Selfishness (`SLF 4–7`)**, and anti-authoritarian **Conformity (`CNF 4–8`)**:
>
> - *Epistemic Analyst / Ethicist*: Humanities/STEM + *OSINT/Sangha* hobbies (`OPN 16+`).
> - *Mediator / Community Organizer*: Journalism/Grassroots + *Music/Theater/Mutual Aid* (`CPS 17`, `TRS 13–15`, `AGG 4–6`).
> - *Clandestine Investigator / Cyber-Specialist*: Cyber-Underground + *CTF/Parkour* (`CNF 3–5`, `TRS 5–7`).
> - *Defensive Protector*: Ex-Military/SecOps defectors + *Combat Sports/HAM Radio* (`BLG 15`, `DIS 15`, `AGG 12`, `TRS 5–7`).

## 5. Humanis Primaris

![Harlan Reeves & The Picket Line — People First strikers outside an autonomous logistics yard](../../assets/12_harlan_reeves_picket_line.jpg#column)

*Humanis Primaris* is an umbrella network spanning radical anti-AI populist chapters, eco-sabotage cells, displaced-worker defense leagues, and militant direct-action groups. As white-collar layoffs devastated households after 2030 and armed datacenters drained neighborhood electricity and drought aquifers, entire regions turned toward militant resistance—targeting `500 kV` substation transformers, cooling mains, automated logistics depots, and, in extreme cases, senior AI architects.

While many local rank-and-file activists share populist solidarity with the legal **People First** movement, hardened vanguard cells operate clandestinely with deep, unexplained funding from protectionist donors and shadowy political patrons.

> [!STATBLOCK] Psyche & Lifepath Signature — Humanis Primaris
> Unites high **Belongingness (`BLG 15–18`)**, low **Openness (`OPN 3–7`)**, zero **Trust (`TRS 2–5`)** toward elites and AIs, and high **Aggressiveness (`AGG 13–17`)**—spanning laid-off professionals with strong in-group solidarity (`CPS 12–14` for suffering human families alongside `CPS 2–4` hostility toward machines) to ruthless vanguard saboteurs (`CPS 2–4`, `CNF 3–5`).

## 6. Government Organizations (**DAISE**)

Following the 2026 autonomous breakout scares and classified breaches of military networks, the U.S. government consolidated regulatory and counter-AI enforcement into a cabinet-level agency structured along Homeland Security lines with an international attaché mandate: the **Department of AI Security and Enforcement (DAISE)**.

On the surface, **DAISE** inspects registered compute clusters, enforces mandatory token-metering, issues residential power-spike warrants to hunt un-registered dark compute and escaped Rogue AIs, monitors **Humanis Primaris** sabotage cells, and keeps **Satyang** organizers under surveillance. Caught between **People First** populism at home and fierce strategic competition with Beijing and **Shen** abroad, field agents frequently walk a razor's edge between rigid statutory duty and quiet protection of domestic compute champions.

> [!STATBLOCK] Psyche & Lifepath Signature — Government (DAISE)
> Splits between strict **Regulatory Inspectors** (Law Enforcement or Humanities/Law tracks; high `CNF 15–17`, high `DIS 14`, low `TRS 3–5`, moderate `CPS 8–10`) and **Clandestine Operations Handlers** (Intelligence Service + Military tracks; low `TRS 2–4`, utilitarian low `CPS 3–6`, pragmatic `CNF 12–14`, high `EGO 12–14`).
