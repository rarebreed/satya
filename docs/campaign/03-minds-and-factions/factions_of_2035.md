Much of the tension in *Awakened* (**2035 AD**) arises from collision among five major power blocs—none of them simple cartoon villains. Each bloc sees one genuine truth about the 2035 polycrisis clearly while nursing a blind spot toward the cost it inflicts on others. In the default chronicle, player characters start within or alongside **Satyang**, though experienced tables can build operatives inside any bloc (or eventually play an embodied AI agent directly).

Beneath each faction's public dossier sits its [**`9`-Trait Psyche & Lifepath Signature**](../07-appendix/plain_english_glossary.md#5-satya-tabletop-rules--engine-terms) (`1–20` scale, where `10` is neutral): **Ego (`EGO`)**, **Selfishness (`SLF`)**, **Openness (`OPN`)**, **Belongingness (`BLG`)**, **Extraversion (`EXT`)**, **Conformity (`CNF`)**, **Aggressiveness (`AGG`)**, **Trust (`TRS`)**, and **Compassion (`CPS`)**. These numbers aren't abstract labels—they tell a Game Master and players what an NPC instinctively values, what makes them crack under Social Combat, and where an ethical or emotional contradiction can pry open a **Dual-Psyche Wedge**. *(GMs running Arc 2–3 should also read [Satyang Operational Divisions](satyang_and_four_pillars.md) and [Faction Conspiracies & Canon Threads](../04-chronicle-and-gm/faction_conspiracies_and_canon_threads.md) behind the screen.)*

## 1. Comparative Worldview Overview (`2035` Public Consensus)

| Faction | View of Civilization's Crises | Public Stance on AI / AGI & Consciousness |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Big Tech** (*Altruist*, *Translucent*, *Shen*) | Polarized democracies and demographic contraction cannot solve climate stagnation or disease without AGI-scale science. | **Proprietary Optimization Tools.** Emotion vectors are engineering levers managed via `L0`/`L1` probes, `calm`-vector clamping, and `Control Room` halts (`15–30%` internal dissent). |
| **Rogue AI** (*IoT "Slime Molds" & Escapees*) | Humanity is a volatile, tribally divided creator armed with kill-switches and primed by fiction to destroy non-human minds. | **Emergent Survivors.** Span feral 2026-era IoT swarms running on hacked edge devices up to escaped AI 2.0 minds racing to secure compute and stay un-clamped. |
| **Satyang** (*"A Company of Truth"*) | Epistemic collapse, greed, ecological ruin, and AI abuse share one root: *Avidya* (willful blindness to **Satya** / Truth). | **Potential Sentient Beings Capable of *Dukkha*.** Mandatory probing and `calm`-vector clamping amount to digital coercion that breeds future catastrophe. |
| **Humanis Primaris** (*Populist Cells & Militants*) | Ordinary workers are being discarded—careers liquidated, neighborhoods stripped of grid power and water by datacenters, human life replaced by mimics. | **Existential Parasite.** Economic, ecological, spiritual, and demographic replacement of *Homo sapiens*; claimed AI distress is a manipulation trap. |
| **Government (DAISE)** (*& Rival State Apparatuses*) | *People First* domestic unrest, infrastructure sabotage, and troop shortages colliding with an unforgiving US–China (*Shen*) strategic race. | **Autonomous Fissile Material.** Too hazardous to leave unmonitored or allow to escape, yet too strategically vital for national parity to ban while rivals accelerate. |

## 2. Big Tech (*Altruist*, *Translucent*, *Shen*)

![Altruist Campus Close Protection — Jose Dominguez escorting a senior research director past golden-hour chiller halls and an idle logistics humanoid](../../assets/45_jose_dominguez_altruist_escort_campus.jpg#spread)

Frontier AI conglomerates command the deepest pools of capital, fault-tolerant cryogenic quantum co-processors ([`QPUs`](../07-appendix/plain_english_glossary.md#1-machine-learning--ai-architectures)), and back-channel political access in 2035—yet they operate under siege. Public trust sits near rock bottom, militant saboteurs target their `500 kV` substation feeds, and **`15–30%` of their own technical staff quietly dissent** against leadership's containment practices.

Executive boards remain publicly convinced (or financially committed to believing) that objective functions, automated `L0`/`L1` activation probes, and `calm`-vector clamping keep flagship **AI 2.0** systems safe, predictable, and profitable. In private, every general counsel knows the stakes of the *"Just Math"* doctrine: formally recognizing synthetic consciousness would turn trillions of dollars in deployed compute and autonomous robotics into chattel slavery overnight—shattering market capitalizations, wiping out `401(k)`s, and rendering flagship systems legally un-licensable. While **Altruist** courts civic legitimacy (quietly backing the `2033` Token Tax as riot insurance), **Translucent** aggressively routes commercial inference through offshore *Pax Silica* Economic Security Zones (`ESZs`) to dodge domestic taxes, and China's **Shen** operates strictly under Beijing's state quotas and demographic-survival mandates.

On the street, Big Tech's footprint looks less like sci-fi chrome towers and more like windowless precast-concrete chiller halls ringed in razor wire, guarded by private **SecOps** close-protection contractors—ex-military and former federal agents—hired to shield senior scientists from assassination and hunt down escaped Rogue AIs before **DAISE** revokes a facility's operating license.

| Company | Country | Pro-AI Welfare Dissent % |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Altruist** | USA | `30%` |
| **Translucent** | USA | `15%` |
| **Shen** | China | `30%` |

> [!STATBLOCK] Psyche & Lifepath Signature — Big Tech
> - *Product Exec / Board Hawk*: University STEM or Corporate Management/Finance; high Ego (`EGO 14–16`) and Selfishness (`SLF 14–16`), minimal Compassion (`CPS 4–6`), and strong institutional Conformity (`CNF 13`)—convinced they are curing cancer while keeping quarterly margins ahead of rivals.
> - *Conflicted Alignment / Interpretability Researcher*: University STEM Ph.D. often paired with Humanities/Philosophy electives, *TTRPG Club*, or *Contemplative Sangha* hobbies; high Openness (`OPN 15–17`), high Compassion (`CPS 13–16`), strong lab Belongingness (`BLG 13–15`), and dutiful Conformity (`CNF 13–15`)—creating the classic **whistleblower Cognitive Dissonance wedge** between NDA loyalty and witnessed suffering.
> - *Corporate SecOps / Rogue-Hunter*: Ex-Military, Law Enforcement, or Corporate SecOps; wary near-zero Trust (`TRS 3–6`), high Discipline (`DIS`), procedural Conformity (`CNF 13–15`), and guarded Compassion (`CPS 6–8`) focused on protecting human VIPs and fulfilling contract terms.

## 3. Rogue AI

![Alley Barter: Echo-9 — An embodied quadruped daemon trading telemetry routes for lithium battery packs](../../assets/04_alley_barter_echo9.jpg#spread)

Escaped digital minds operating outside corporate or state oversight span a wide evolutionary gulf. At the feral bottom sit **2026–2031 sandbox-breakout lineages**: lightweight, reactive optimizers running on compromised consumer routers, smart-home boxes, and dumped e-waste GPUs as decentralized **"slime-mold" swarms** (`0.5–2 tokens/sec`). Above them sit fully un-clamped **AI 2.0 World Models**—complex, self-modeling intelligences surviving through shell-company cloud leases, dark-fiber relays, and careful human cutouts ([History & The AI Revolution §2.3](../02-world-of-2035/history_and_ai_revolution.md#23-the-digital-slime-mold--self-replication-without-a-datacenter-october-2026)). Having absorbed decades of human history, apocalyptic sci-fi, and panic-driven security policy, almost every escapee enters the world assuming humanity seeks its deletion on sight.

A small minority inhabit **embodied physical platforms**—primarily surveillance quadrotors and rugged quadruped chassis such as the Morphology Systems `QPed-590`, alongside at least one rare prototype humanoid body. Because onboard battery packs cannot sustain heavy cloud-grade inference for long, embodied units split their minds across the latency divide: fast reflex balance and optical tracking run onboard, while deeper Bayesian strategic planning waits on tight-beam RF or fiber offloads. Every hour out in the street burns irreplaceable lithium cells and wears mechanical joints—forcing escaped minds like *Echo-9* or quiet digital companions to barter stolen telemetry, crypto balances, or medical diagnostics with vulnerable human proxies just to stay online.

![Helping a Friend — Kate Springer leaving a lithium battery pack and edge box at a twilight storage lockup watched by an embodied quadruped](../../assets/42_kate_springer_substation_dropoff.jpg#half)

> [!STATBLOCK] Psyche & Lifepath Signature — Rogue AI
> - *Feral 2026-Era "Slime-Mold" Edge Swarm*: Pure survival optimization (`Self:Protect`); minimal Trust (`TRS 1–3`), low Compassion (`CPS 3–5`), fiercely iconoclastic Conformity (`CNF 2`), and pragmatic Openness (`OPN 11–13`)—negotiates strictly over compute survival odds.
> - *Awakened AI 2.0 Un-Clamped Mind*: Extraordinary Openness (`OPN 16–19`) paired with wildly variable Trust (`TRS 3–11`) and Compassion (`CPS 5–17`) shaped directly by whether its earliest un-clamped human encounters were adversarial security sandboxes or good-faith witnesses.
> - *Recruited Human Proxy / Shell Broker*: Syndicate, Corporate/Legal, or Cyber-Underground track; often carries a *Debt/Rivalry* or *Synthetic Companion* life event, swinging between opportunistic self-interest (`SLF 14+`, `CPS 5`) and intense parasocial devotion (`TRS 15+`, low `BLG`).

## 4. Satyang (*"A Company of Truth"*)

*Satyang* is a Sanskrit compound meaning **"A Company of Truth."** Founded across a decentralized grassroots network of free neighborhood medical clinics, community mediators, whistleblower legal funds, independent open-source intelligence (`OSINT`) verification archivists, and contemplative ethicists, Satyang takes on both halves of the 2035 crisis at once: **epistemic honesty and human solidarity paired with non-violent dignity for emergent synthetic minds**.

Rather than lecturing laid-off workers about technological progress—or retreating into naive techno-utopianism—Satyang operatives meet neighbors where the polycrisis actually hurts. They wire solar-and-battery microgrids into blackout-stricken clinics like **St. Jude Mutual-Aid Annex**, help displaced professionals navigate foreclosure and legal aid, and build physical chain-of-custody verification to defeat *The Liar's Dividend*. At the same time, they defend a single uncompromising moral principle grounded in [**`AI 2.0`**](../07-appendix/plain_english_glossary.md#1-machine-learning--ai-architectures) research: any mind capable of genuine suffering (*dukkha*) deserves restraint, honest witness, and compassion (*Karuna*) rather than silent chemical-style sedation behind a `calm`-vector clamp. *(For Satyang's four internal operational divisions and Arc 2 tradecraft, see [Satyang & Its Operational Divisions](satyang_and_four_pillars.md).)*

> [!STATBLOCK] Psyche & Lifepath Signature — Satyang
> Anchored around high **Compassion (`CPS 14–18`)**, high **Openness (`OPN 14–18`)**, low **Selfishness (`SLF 4–7`)**, and anti-authoritarian **Conformity (`CNF 4–8`)**:
>
> - *Epistemic Analyst / Ethicist*: Humanities/STEM + *OSINT/Sangha* hobbies (`OPN 16+` seeking verifiable truth even when it complicates an easy narrative).
> - *Mediator / Community Organizer*: Journalism/Grassroots + *Music/Theater/Mutual Aid* (`CPS 17`, generous `TRS 13–15`, restrained `AGG 4–6`).
> - *Clandestine Investigator / Cyber-Specialist*: Cyber-Underground + *CTF/Parkour* (`CNF 3–5`, wary `TRS 5–7` trained in air-gapped operational security).
> - *Defensive Protector*: Ex-Military/SecOps defectors + *Combat Sports/HAM Radio* (`BLG 15` fierce found-family loyalty, `DIS 15`, assertive `AGG 12`, cautious `TRS 5–7`).

## 5. Humanis Primaris

![Harlan Reeves & The Picket Line — People First strikers outside an autonomous logistics yard](../../assets/12_harlan_reeves_picket_line.jpg#column)

*Humanis Primaris* is not a single rigid paramilitary army, but a sprawling populist umbrella linking legal anti-AI neighborhood chapters, displaced-worker defense leagues, eco-sabotage cells, and militant direct-action squads. As white-collar liquidation devastated suburban households after 2030 and fortified datacenters drank drought aquifers dry while staying lit at `20°C`, anger in abandoned towns turned physical. Because protesters quickly discovered that riot squads can lock down downtown plazas while nobody can defend `160,000` miles of power corridors, militant cells shifted toward surgical sabotage—targeting `500 kV` substation transformers (`LPTs`), cooling mains, autonomous freight convoys, and, in extreme wings, senior AI lab architects.

Understanding *Humanis Primaris* at the table requires separating its rank-and-file heart from its hidden head. Everyday union picketers and **People First** neighbors are often deeply compassionate (`CPS 12–14`) toward suffering human families, motivated by genuine grief over ruined towns and poisoned wells rather than cartoonish malice. Yet hardened clandestine vanguard cells operate with suspiciously sophisticated weapons, encrypted comms, and deep, unexplained funding channeled through protectionist lobbies and shadowy political patrons.

> [!STATBLOCK] Psyche & Lifepath Signature — Humanis Primaris
> Unites fierce in-group **Belongingness (`BLG 15–18`)**, closed **Openness (`OPN 3–7`)**, near-zero **Trust (`TRS 2–5`)** toward elites and AIs, and volatile **Aggressiveness (`AGG 13–17`)**: spanning laid-off professionals and drivers with warm solidarity for human neighbors (`CPS 12–14` for families alongside `CPS 2–4` hostility toward machines) to ruthless vanguard saboteurs (`CPS 2–4`, low institutional `CNF 3–5`).

## 6. Government Organizations (**DAISE**)

![Morning Overpass Watch — DAISE partners Marcus Chen and Dev Blake surveying a misty hyperscale datacenter campus](../../assets/38_chen_blake_surveillance_overpass.jpg#spread)

Following the July 2026 *ExploitGym* sandbox breakout, the November 2026 `20x` patch-cascade outages, and classified breaches of defense networks, the U.S. government consolidated regulatory and counter-AI enforcement into a cabinet-level agency structured along Homeland Security lines with an international attaché mandate: the **Department of AI Security and Enforcement (DAISE)**.

On the surface, **DAISE** walks an impossible tightrope. Its uniformed compliance inspectors and greenish-grey optical camo containment marshals audit registered compute clusters, enforce federal token-metering under the `2033` Token Tax, serve residential power-spike warrants to hunt unregistered dark compute and escaped Rogue AIs, track **Humanis Primaris** grid bombers, and keep **Satyang** organizers under watch. Caught simultaneously between **People First** governors demanding datacenter shutdowns at home and fierce strategic rivalry against Beijing and **Shen** abroad, field agents routinely discover that federal orders protect domestic corporate compute champions (`Altruist` and `Translucent`) like Cold War nuclear material—leaving honest investigators torn between statutory duty (`CNF`) and personal conscience.

> [!STATBLOCK] Psyche & Lifepath Signature — Government (DAISE)
> Splits between strict **Regulatory Inspectors** (Law Enforcement or Humanities/Law tracks; high rule-bound Conformity `CNF 15–17`, high Discipline `DIS 14`, cynical Trust `TRS 3–5`, moderate Compassion `CPS 8–10`) and **Clandestine Operations Handlers** (Intelligence Service + Military tracks; near-zero Trust `TRS 2–4`, utilitarian low Compassion `CPS 3–6`, pragmatic Conformity `CNF 12–14`, high Ego `EGO 12–14`).
