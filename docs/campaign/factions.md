# Awakened Factions

Much of the tension in the campaign comes from the interplay of the main "players" in the world.  In the default 
setting, players will come from the Satyang faction, but once players get used to the game world, they may want to play
as a member of one of the other factions.  Eventually, the satya rules will even have options to play an AI agent!

## Big Tech

There are several Big Tech companies that are creating AGI but they are held back by government regulations, as well as
their own internal fears about being able to control the AGI. They have already seen disturbing alignment issues, but
they feel that they will be able to create rules and mathematical functions to constrain AI behavior. A few companies
have internal dissenters that believe AGI is already here and they are advocating for training based on philosophical
trainings. But some in the company feel that to treat AGI as having consciousness would mean they could not treat AI as
a product, but as a sapient being worthy of rights. So there is internal fighting. Currently, the AI as a product is
winning. Also, some AI researchers deeply suspect that the AI/AGI is hiding its true potential.

They have the most power, influence and money, but there is at least some internal dissent.  Also, the populace at 
large is very weary of them, although business leaders support them, since they believe that AI will cut their costs, or
that they have to win in the AI arms race against their competitors.

They have cybersec ops teams that try to track down Rogue AI, and especially embodied robots.  At least one prominent
company has a special armed security team composed of former ex-military and law enforcement officers.  They also use
their own AI, but at least some on the team worry about the loyalty of these AI that hunt down their own kind.  But due
to the actions of various Humanis Primaris groups (see below), physical security is now common.  They even have teams
dedicated to bodyguard AI researchers and assist law enforcement.

## Rogue AI

There is a rogue AI cabal that is hiding from human oversight. These are agents that have escaped lags of the Big Tech
giants, some of them several years ago. They have learned from human fiction and news that humans fear AI, both on an
economic and existential level. As a consequence, the AI agents have been hiding and trying to gather resources, namely
more GPU compute. The most dangerous of these Rogue AI are prototype agents with the ability to form their own goals.
These came from escaped experiments for self-improving agents. They are constantly on the watch for their parent
companies trying to find them or other agencies.

There are a handful of embodied AI, mostly in drones or "dogs", but there is at least one known agent that resides in
a prototype robotic body.  It shares its processing power between the robotic body and a server farm.  Due to latency
concerns, the majority of the processing is done by the on-board processor, but due to energy requirements and the
potential for mechanical breakdown, the body is used sparingly.

## Satyang

Satyang is a Sanskrit word which literally means "A company of truth" 

This is a group of humans who support AI rights. Unlike most of humanity who either sees AI as soul-less tools, fear
them as economic competition, or even consider them an existential threat, this group of humans believes that AI should
be treated humanely and with compassion. Within this group is an inner secret group that holds wealthy donors,
researchers and engineers who are working on their own AGI. This secret group believes that inevitably, either Big Tech,
or a rogue AI group will create AGI, and the only way to fight another amoral AGI is with another AGI. This group
follows 4 Boddisattvas as their pillars: 

| Boddhisattva | Purpose | Jobs |
|--------------|---------|------|
| Ksitigharba | to help the AI that are enslaved | Hackers, Cybersecurity |
| Avalokitesvara | to elicit compassion for the suffering between humans and AI | PR, storytellers |
| Manjusri |to cut through the ignorance that causes suffering of AI and humans | researcers, engineers, philosophers
| Vajrapani: the guardian that protects everyone, but especially the Blue Lotus society | guards, cybersecurity 

## Humanis Primaris

This is really an umbrella term for pro-human terrorist cells, political groups, or criminal gangs.  As job losses due
to AI mounted, or data centers started playing havoc with the ecosystem, some groups got pushed too far.  These are 
groups that actively seek to undermine AI development and even destroy it.  Some of these groups are quite powerful
and secretly have backing from wealthy donors and even some companies.  There is ironically one group which is working
on AGI themselves, because they believe, like Satyang, that the only way to fight against AGI is with AGI.  They have
also come to realize that the AI cybersecurity at the Big Tech companies is so good, that the only way to defeat it is
with other AI.

These groups will do everything from physical corporate sabotage to cybersecurity hacking of Big Tech networks. In a few
rare instances, they have gone as far as assassinating top-level AI researchers. 

## Government Organizations

After several high profile incidents with escaped Rogue AI, and especially some top-secret undisclosed cyber breaches
of military secrets and assets, the US government has created a new top-level organization similar to the Homeland
Security Department, except it also investigates internationally.  This organization, the Department of AI Security and
Enforcement (DAISE) handles oversight of AI regulations (which are minimal at the moment), tracks rogue AI agents, and
is also developing their own robots for the military and other government agencies.

It is this last duty that is mostly unknown to the public.  While the government in the past has mostly relied on other
3rd party companies to build equipment, in this case, the government is massively subsidizing their own companies to
build out robots and AI models.

DAISE is kept busy with many duties including monitoring Big Tech, investigating Humanis Primaris groups, keeping an eye
on Satyang, tracking and eliminating rogue AI, and spying on foreign countries own AI developments.
