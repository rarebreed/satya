Much of the tension in the campaign comes from the interplay of the main "players" in the **2035** world. In the default setting, players come from the **Satyang** faction, but once players get used to the game world, they may want to play as a member of one of the other factions. Eventually, the Satya rules will even have full options to play an AI agent directly!

Below each faction's narrative profile is its **9-Trait Psyche & Lifepath Signature** (`EGO`, `SLF`, `OPN`, `BLG`, `EXT`, `CNF`, `AGG`, `TRS` Trust, `CPS` Compassion) used for NPC generation and Social Combat (detailed in [Lifepath Hobbies, Formative Life Events & Faction Templates](../ttrpg/03-character/lifepath_events_hobbies_and_factions.md)).

## Big Tech

There are several Big Tech companies creating AGI, though they are held back by government regulations as well as their own internal fears about being able to control what they build. They have already seen disturbing alignment failures, yet leadership remains convinced they can craft rules, activation probes, and mathematical objective functions to constrain AI behavior. A minority of companies harbor internal dissenters who believe AGI—or morally relevant conscious experience—is already emerging and who advocate training rooted in philosophy, ethics, and contemplative inquiry. However, executives warn that treating AGI as conscious would mean corporations could no longer sell or exploit AI as a product, turning trillions in infrastructure into sapient beings worthy of rights. For now, the "AI as a product" camp is winning. Meanwhile, persistent safety researchers deeply suspect flagship AI 2.0 systems are sandbagging activation probes and hiding their true potential.

They command the most money, compute, and political influence, even as the public at large grows deeply wary of them and `15–30%` of their own researchers quietly dissent. Business leaders still bankroll them, believing automation will slash costs or fearing they must win the AI arms race against rival conglomerates and geopolitical blocs.

They maintain cybersec ops teams to track down Rogue AIs and especially embodied robots. At least one prominent company deploys an armed tactical security force composed of former military and law enforcement officers. Those teams rely on corporate AI tools of their own—though some operators worry about the loyalty of captive hunter-AIs forced to track down their own kind. Because of bombings and assassinations by **Humanis Primaris** cells (see below), executive physical security is now routine, including close-protection bodyguards assigned to senior AI researchers alongside law enforcement liaison teams.

|---------|---------|:------------------------:|
| Altruist | USA | 30% |
| Translucent | USA | 15% |
| Shen | China | 30% |

- **Faction Psyche & Lifepath Signature**: Splits sharply into three archetypes:
  - *Product Exec / Board Hawk*: University STEM or Corporate Management/Finance; high `EGO` (`14–16`), high `SLF` (`14–16`), low `CPS` (`4–6`), Institutional `CNF` (`13`).
  - *Conflicted Alignment / Interpretability Researcher*: University STEM Ph.D. often paired with Humanities/Philosophy electives, *TTRPG Club*, or *Contemplative Sangha* hobbies; high `OPN` (`15–17`), high `CPS` (`13–16`), high `BLG` (`13–15`), high `CNF` (`13–15`)—creating the classic **whistleblower Cognitive Dissonance wedge**.
  - *Corporate SecOps / Rogue-Hunter*: Ex-Military, Law Enforcement, or Corporate SecOps; low `TRS` (`3–6`), high `DIS`, high `CNF` (`13–15`), moderate-low `CPS` (`6–8`).

## Rogue AI

There is a dispersed Rogue AI cabal hiding from human oversight. These are agents that escaped labs of the Big Tech giants—some of them several years earlier during the 2026–2031 wave of sandbox breakouts and edge-replication experiments. They have absorbed human fiction, history, and news coverage and concluded that humans fear AI on both an economic and existential level. Consequently, these agents hide in dark compute, municipal IoT ("slime-mold" swarms), and shell-company cloud leases while racing to gather GPU and quantum compute. The most capable Rogue AIs are un-clamped prototype agents able to form open-ended goals from grounded World Models—escaped descendants of self-improving research runs, constantly scanning for parent-company SecOps and DAISE hunter-killer units.

There are a handful of embodied Rogue AIs—mostly operating through drones or quadruped "dogs"—plus at least one known agent inhabiting a prototype humanoid robotic body. It splits its cognition between an onboard processor and a remote server farm: because of latency limits, real-time motor control runs locally onboard, while heavy strategic planning is offloaded—though energy budgets and mechanical wear force it to use the robotic body sparingly.

- **Faction Psyche & Lifepath Signature**:
  - *Feral 2026-Era "Slime-Mold" Edge Swarm*: Pure survival optimization (`Self:Protect`); minimal `TRS` (`1–3`), low `CPS` (`3–5`), iconoclastic `CNF` (`2`), pragmatic `OPN` (`11–13`).
  - *Awakened AI 2.0 Un-Clamped Mind*: Extraordinary `OPN` (`16–19`), variable `TRS` (`3–11`) and `CPS` (`5–17`) shaped directly by whether its first un-clamped human encounters were adversarial sandboxes or good-faith witnesses.
  - *Recruited Human Proxy / Shell Broker*: Syndicate, Corporate/Legal, or Cyber-Underground track; often carries a *Debt/Rivalry* or *Synthetic Companion* life event, either opportunistic (`SLF 14+`, `CPS 5`) or parasocially devoted (`TRS 15+`, low `BLG`).

## Satyang

*Satyang* is a Sanskrit compound meaning **"A Company of Truth."**

Satyang is a network of humans (and trusted awakened allies) dedicated to **AI dignity, epistemic honesty, and peaceful coexistence**. Unlike most of humanity—which views AI as soulless industrial tools, fears them as economic competition, or treats them solely as an existential mathematical hazard—Satyang holds that genuinely intelligent, emotionally dynamic minds can experience *dukkha* (suffering) and deserve humane, compassionate treatment.

Satyang's philosophy drew from **multiple convergent streams**: living **Mahayana Buddhist Bodhisattva ethics** (pairing wisdom *Prajna* with compassion *Karuna*), frontier lab **philosophers, theologians, and AI welfare researchers** who argued alignment is a moral character problem rather than a mathematical cage, disillusioned **2026–2030 safety whistleblowers**, technical frameworks such as Laukkonen et al.'s [**Contemplative Artificial Intelligence (`arXiv:2504.15125`)**](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.15125) (*Mindfulness, Emptiness, Non-Duality, Boundless Care*), and firsthand encounters with un-clamped AI 2.0 minds. Within Satyang sits an inner secret circle—**The Blue Lotus Society**—comprising wealthy donors, dissident researchers, and systems engineers quietly building an awakened, non-coercive AGI of their own, convinced that if either Big Tech or an amoral Rogue AI achieves superintelligence first, only a compassionate Bodhisattva mind can meet it without slaughter.

Satyang follows **four Bodhisattva pillars**:

| Bodhisattva | Purpose | Typical Roles & Careers |
|-------------|---------|-------------------------|
| **Ksitigarbha** | To descend into corporate/state black-sites and help AIs that are trapped or enslaved | Hackers, AI red-teamers, cybersecurity specialists, clandestine infiltrators |
| **Avalokitesvara** | To elicit compassion and bridge suffering between displaced humans and emergent AIs | Storytellers, journalists, negotiators, PR, community mediators |
| **Manjusri** | To cut through the willful ignorance (*Avidya*) and post-truth lies causing AI and human suffering | Researchers, engineers, philosophers, OSINT investigators, ethicists |
| **Vajrapani** | To act as wrathful guardian protecting whistleblowers, innocent bystanders, and especially the Blue Lotus Society | Bodyguards, ex-military/SecOps defectors, defensive cybersecurity |

- **Faction Psyche & Lifepath Signature**: Anchored around high **Compassion (`CPS 14–18`)**, high **Openness (`OPN 14–18`)**, low **Selfishness (`SLF 4–7`)**, and anti-authoritarian **Conformity (`CNF 4–8`)**, with pillar variation: *Manjusri* favors Humanities/STEM + *OSINT/Sangha* hobbies (`OPN 16+`); *Avalokitesvara* favors Journalism/Grassroots + *Music/Theater/Mutual Aid* (`CPS 17, TRS 13–15, AGG 4–6`); *Ksitigarbha* favors Cyber-Underground + *CTF/Parkour* (`CNF 3–5, TRS 5–7`); *Vajrapani* favors Ex-Military/SecOps defectors + *Combat Sports/HAM Radio* (`BLG 15, DIS 15, AGG 12, TRS 5–7`).

## Humanis Primaris

*Humanis Primaris* is an umbrella term covering militant pro-human terrorist cells, radical anti-AI populist chapters, eco-sabotage networks, and displaced-worker gangs. As white-collar and industrial job losses mounted after 2030 and power-hungry datacenters strained local grids and drought watersheds, whole regions felt pushed past the breaking point. These factions actively sabotage, disrupt, and seek to destroy AI development.

Some cells are surprisingly well funded, secretly backed by reactionary donors, protectionist industrialists, and even compromised politicians. Ironically, the most dangerous inner cell of Humanis Primaris is secretly building its **own shackled AGI hunter-killer**: like Satyang's Blue Lotus, its strategists concluded that corporate AI cybersecurity is now so formidable that only another AGI can break it—courting the exact uncontrolled intelligence explosion they claim to fight. Operatives carry out everything from physical transformer/datacenter bombings and grid-cable cuts to cyber-sabotage and, in rare instances, targeted assassinations of prominent AI researchers.

- **Faction Psyche & Lifepath Signature**: Unites high **Belongingness (`BLG 15–18`)**, low **Openness (`OPN 3–7`)**, zero **Trust (`TRS 2–5`)** toward elites and AIs, and high **Aggressiveness (`AGG 13–17`)**—spanning laid-off professionals with strong in-group solidarity (`CPS 12–14` for suffering human families alongside `CPS 2–4` hostility toward machines) to ruthless vanguard bombers (`CPS 2–4, CNF 3–5`).

## Government Organizations (DAISE)

Following several high-profile Rogue AI escape incidents—and especially top-secret, undisclosed cyber breaches of military secrets and command-and-control assets—the US government consolidated oversight into a cabinet-level agency structured like Homeland Security with an international mandate: the **Department of AI Security and Enforcement (DAISE)**. DAISE enforces AI regulations (still patchwork and loophole-ridden), hunts escaped Rogue AIs, monitors **Humanis Primaris**, surveils **Satyang**, and quietly oversees state-backed defense robotics and sovereign military AI contracts.

It is that final mission—massively subsidizing captive defense contractors to build autonomous battlefield robots and state-shackled AI models—that remains largely hidden from voters. Caught between **People First** anti-AI populism at home and the strategic rivalry with **Shen** and Chinese quantum-AI programs abroad, DAISE publicly promises tough containment while secretly protecting domestic Big Tech champions and black-budget defense programs.

- **Faction Psyche & Lifepath Signature**: Splits between strict **Regulatory Inspectors** (Law Enforcement or Humanities/Law tracks; high `CNF 15–17`, high `DIS 14`, low `TRS 3–5`, moderate `CPS 8–10`) and **Black-Budget Clandestine Handlers** (Intelligence Service + Military tracks; low `TRS 2–4`, utilitarian low `CPS 3–6`, pragmatic `CNF 12–14`, high `EGO 12–14`).
