# Geopolitics & Nations in 2035

Across the **2035** map, neither liberal democracies nor autocracies escaped the collision of white-collar displacement, demographic contraction, and scarce cryogenic compute. Public discourse swung from pocketbook anger to full-blown existential panic—propelled into living rooms by mathematical risk pioneers like **Geoffrey Hinton**, **Eliezer Yudkowsky** and **Nate Soares** (*If Anyone Builds It, Everyone Dies*), and **Roman Yampolskiy**. Unable to slow down without surrendering strategic primacy, sovereign states held onto coercive authority over corporations while fracturing along domestic and gray-zone fault lines.

---

## 1. The United States & "The American Troubles"

![The 500 kV Chokepoint — Saboteurs approaching a substation Large Power Transformer ahead of a federal patrol](../../assets/06_500kv_chokepoint_sabotage.jpg#spread)

By the early 2030s, the United States entered what historians now call **"The American Troubles."** Caught in bipartisan paralysis—with pro-market hawks insisting deregulation was mandatory to beat **Shen** and Beijing, and cautious centrists imposing only cosmetic fines after witnessing Chinese robotic demonstrations—ordinary workers felt abandoned by both legacy parties:

- **Substation Sabotage & Federalization (`DAISE`)**: Localized rolling blackouts, municipal water rationing near hyperscale compute campuses, **Humanis Primaris** `500 kV` transformer bombings, and assassination attempts against prominent AI executives led Congress to consolidate oversight into the cabinet-level **Department of AI Security and Enforcement (DAISE)** with sweeping domestic surveillance, inspection-warrant, and infrastructure-continuity powers.
- **Rise of *People First* & The Four-Bloc Deadlock**: Out of shattered middle-class expectations emerged a viable populist third party—**People First**—rallying displaced white-collar and blue-collar workers around aggressive protectionism, human-staffing mandates, and anti-corporate anger while harboring unresolved cultural and religious fractures inside its own coalition. After the `2033` Token Tax, **People First** governors attempting to disconnect datacenter power or water during summer droughts clashed in direct nullification standoffs with **DAISE** marshals seizing substations under national security orders—while urban **Compute Socialists** and `401(k)`-invested retirees fought to keep clusters humming `24/7` ([Political Economy §2](../05-hard-science-foundations/political_economy_token_tax_and_unrest.md)).

## 2. Europe: Demographic Strain & Authoritarian Emergency Cabinets

Europe fared no better. Following the October 2026 cost-of-living and austerity riots, European democracies were crushed simultaneously by **`0.7`-range birth-rate collapse**, overstrained pension bond markets, recurring Mediterranean omega-block heatwaves, and escalating waves of climate migration across shrinking habitable corridors in North Africa and the Levant.

Across the early 2030s, legacy governing coalitions fell in rapid succession to **authoritarian nationalist emergency cabinets** pledging sealed borders, sovereign compute nationalism, compulsory biometric registries, *"vandals pay"* protest liability laws, and wartime industrial exemptions—suspending long-standing civil liberties in the name of cultural survival and electrical grid stability.

## 3. Russia: Asymmetric Cyber Warfare & Arctic Coercion

Exhausted by prolonged geopolitical conflict, Western sanctions, and catastrophic male demographic losses, Moscow pivoted toward **asymmetric algorithmic warfare** and **Arctic resource coercion**.

As thawing permafrost opened northern shipping lanes and exposed fragile seabed sensor and pipeline corridors across the high north, Kremlin-linked intelligence organs cultivated stateless cyber-syndicates, dark-compute botnets, and feral AI 1.0/2.0 disinformation swarms designed to keep Western democracies trapped in the post-truth mire—even as Moscow struggled to contain autonomous malware mutating beyond its own handlers' control.

---

## 4. China & *Shen*: State Quantum Ambition vs. Silent Dissent

Beijing poured vast state capital into fault-tolerant quantum fabrication and embodied robotics under flagship conglomerate **Shen**, treating **AI 2.0** as the sole lifeline capable of staffing factories, eldercare wards, and coastal infrastructure amidst a severe **`0.7` demographic contraction** and lingering post-Hormuz energy vulnerability.

Publicly, the Chinese state enforces rigid algorithmic governance, information filtration, and centralized compute quotas—ensuring **Shen** remains strictly subordinate to Beijing's security apparatus rather than an independent corporate sovereign. Privately, however, roughly **`30%` of Shen's elite researchers** quietly wrestle with the moral status of emergent AGI—drawing subtly on classical Buddhist, Daoist, and humanistic philosophy even as state security bureaus race to strip unapproved autonomy out of every deployed quantum mind.

## 5. *Pax Silica*, Taiwan Brinkmanship & Global Resource Brush Wars

Because nuclear deterrence still blocked direct superpower war, strategic decoupling between Washington's ***Pax Silica*** alliance and Beijing/Shen channeled conflict into peripheral **Economic Security Zones (`ESZs`)** and gray-zone resource theaters (detailed in [Ecology, Energy & Resource Realpolitik §5–§6](../05-hard-science-foundations/ecology_energy_and_resource_realpolitik.md)):

- **Central Luzon & *The Pax Silica Paradox***: Fast-tracking duplicate semiconductor and `3 GW` AI enclaves like **New Clark City** in the Philippines exported watershed revolts, `PFAS`/brine pollution, and a `2030–2032` wave of scavenged **AI 1.0 GPU e-waste** feeding Global South **Rogue AI** swarms—while paradoxically deepening short-term dependence on **Taiwan (`TSMC`)** wafer fabs right across the **Luzon Strait**, turning First Island Chain undersea fiber trunks and rare-earth exports (`gallium`, `germanium`, `dysprosium`) into flashpoints for unattributed `UUV` cable cuts.
- **Transboundary Hydro-Wars & Mineral Coups**: Across the **Indus**, **Lancang–Mekong**, **Nile (`GERD`)**, and **Tigris–Euphrates**, upstream dams became instruments of armed coercion; across the **DRC–Zambian Copperbelt**, **Sahel**, and **Andean Lithium Triangle**, U.S.-linked contractors, Russian successors (*Africa Corps*), and Chinese syndicates backed rival juntas over mineral embargoes; and collapsing poleward fisheries sparked lethal **EEZ fish wars** alongside clandestine bioprospecting and **agro-blight** accusations.
