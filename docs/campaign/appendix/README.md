The core *Awakened* campaign documents are written to keep game sessions fast, dramatic, and intuitive at the table. This **`appendix/`** directory collects the deeper technical essays, physics mechanisms, formal logic, and real-world scholarly lineages behind the 2035 setting—giving GMs, players, and AI storytellers rigorous conceptual grounding without cluttering baseline lore chapters.

Because the engine's lore retrievers index every Markdown document under `docs/campaign/`, material placed here remains discoverable during campaign prep while keeping core chapters readable on a single pass.

  Contrasts *Satya*'s simulationist design, CARPG vs. pure pen-and-paper execution, cascading `2d20` math, `0.25s` OODA ticks, `T/C/F` Triad, and *Awakened*'s **2035 AD** polycrisis (including why the Quantum [No-Cloning Theorem](https://www.quera.com/glossary/no-cloning-theorem), *anatta*, and *anicca* reject "mind downloading" and consciousness backups) against mainstream RPGs (*D&D 5e*, *Pathfinder 2e*, *Call of Cthulhu / BRP*, *GURPS 4e*, *Phoenix Command*, *Blades in the Dark / PbtA*, *Shadowrun*, *Modiphius `2d20`*) and near-future / sci-fi benchmarks (*Cyberpunk RED*, *CY_BORG*, *Eclipse Phase*, *Steve Jackson's Transhuman Space*, *Traveller*, *Twilight: 2000*, and *Blade Runner RPG*).
